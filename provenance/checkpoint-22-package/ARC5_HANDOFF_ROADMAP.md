## Why illi chooses Coherence Prime

Arc Five makes a group-healing limitation visible.

illi's sustain works very well when Kira absorbs most pressure, but Dungeons/Raids create distributed damage:
- sloppy positioning;
- failed role execution;
- multiple simultaneous injuries;
- Kira pouncing away from the formation;
- longer Green-level attrition.

illi must juggle:
- Persistent Coherence;
- Absorption Shield;
- Genesis Beam;
- emergency reserve.

Her fatigue ceiling is not automatically "the healer's fault"; poor group execution can manufacture excessive healing demand.

Coherence Prime is attractive because it approximates a second autonomous helkir/healing actor and distributes sustain workload.
The need is meaningful but not urgent enough to force immediate purchase.

## Dungeon track

break ranged centers, disperse 3–4 groups when possible, let the party advance through degraded encounters, then reverse into a pincer.

Exact first Green clear/date remains OPEN.

This increased group difficulty exposes illi's distributed-healing/fatigue problem and motivates the G6D2 Coherence Prime purchase.

## Social thesis

Arc Four opened the peer-social door.

Elara cautions Kira against burnout:

Combat continues, but not every fight needs page-time.

## Friendship/group ecology

Five-person Dungeons are the strongest friendship engine:
Kira+illi repeatedly meet three other participants, re-invite good fits and gradually form overlapping cliques rather than a rigid permanent five-person team.
Raids broaden the acquaintance graph.

Trio is deliberately not pursued merely because it exists.
Kira+illi are already top-end Duo performers; a weaker random third increases encounter load and acts as an anchor.
A future recurring Trio partner should emerge organically from friendship, not be invented to fill a leaderboard slot.

## First-cycle demographic / longevity direction

Authorial social center of gravity:
- dense first-cycle population roughly 20–40;
- ordinary working envelope roughly 17–45;
- older first-cycle outliers absolutely exist (including 50s/60s).

This is a distribution, not an eligibility law.

Entering Valnak earlier and developing Bindings/Genesis architecture begins the body's long-term Genesis-field adaptation earlier, contributing to slower aging/longevity.
Exact biology, rate and lifespan effects remain OPEN.
A first entrant at 40 still looks like a 40-year-old and is culturally/developmentally later to the curve than someone who entered young, without being barred from excellence.

## Macro-book scale

Valnak is only the first major domain/setting of Book One — roughly the first third in the current author model.

The Armor/system integration that later resembles a full integrated battle system only begins to reveal itself near the end of the Valnak cycle and continues developing afterward.
