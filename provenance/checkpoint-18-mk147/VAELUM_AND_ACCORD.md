## vaelum

`aithren` is the individual/cultural participation identity. `vaelum` is the persistent organizational layer under which aithren may operate. Both are lowercase ordinary nouns: A-I-T-H-R-E-N and V-A-E-L-U-M.

A vaelum is independent, formally recognized, and contract-capable. It is not a Crown military unit, a noble house, or a geographic place. Members may work through a vaelum while remaining independent operators.

## Accord Registry

The Accord Registry is a Crown administrative domain. Its established purpose is to register and formally recognize vaelum.

Administrative relationship: Crown → Accord Registry → recognized vaelum → aithren members/teams. This describes recognition and organizational relationships; it does not put all aithren under Crown military command.

Other Crown domains may use that recognized status when applying lawful benefits. The Registry is not assigned broader authority or a complete legal framework by this increment. King's Highway and old Accord travel architecture are not imported.

## Economic and tax doctrine

The Crown benefits from decentralized civilian/aithren capability without placing all aithren on permanent government payroll. Recognized vaelum may receive qualifying economic benefits or tax reductions through a distributed Crown incentive model.

Accepted examples include reduced taxation on qualifying harvested resources such as Genesis Dust recovered from ELDRIS; property-related reductions or credits for qualifying headquarters/facilities; and housing/facility benefits associated with maintaining an operational organization.

A vaelum with approximately 200 members may own or lease a large headquarters/residential building and receive a modest qualifying property-related reduction. This is an example, not a required organizational size or an exact tax rule. Rates, qualifying categories, and a complete tax code remain OPEN.

## Operational and contract doctrine

A vaelum serves as a private capability market and fills temporary or specialist civic needs. Its work extends beyond monster hunting.

Potential clients include the Crown, nobles, cities, merchants, caravans, Blackstone, other Institutions of Knowledge, researchers, scholars, physicians, private citizens, and other lawful institutions.

The accepted operational envelope includes:

- Security, bandit suppression, caravan/merchant escort, and temporary guard forces.
- Dungeon escort, Genesis-storm/domai support, and ELDRIS suppression.
- Exploration, retrieval, recovery, harvesting, and rare-material acquisition.
- Investigation, dangerous transportation, and logistics.
- Civil assistance and medical, agricultural, or infrastructure support.
- Unusual one-off environmental/event operations.

For a dungeon research expedition, the researcher controls the research objective. A hired vaelum may supply navigation, route selection, security, ELDRIS response, equipment/logistics, safe working areas, sample/material recovery, and emergency extraction. The vaelum fills the operational capability gap.

## Contracts and reputation

Contracts and organizational reputation are accepted. Demonstrated competence may influence access to higher-risk or higher-value contracts. Exact contract-board systems, arbitration, reputation scoring, and internal vaelum governance remain OPEN. No standardized quest-board bureaucracy is established.
