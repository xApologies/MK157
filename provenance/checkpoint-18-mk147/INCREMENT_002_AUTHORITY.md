## Terminology

| Term | Accepted meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `velis` — V-E-L-I-S | Localized, temporarily persistent Genesis-field topology formed directly by an Architect. “Anchor” is author/workshop shorthand. |
| `vaen` — V-A-E-N | Naturally occurring specialty resources with Genesis-relevant identities/properties; replaces the fantasy-resource use of “elements.” |
| `vaelle` — V-A-E-L-L-E | Nobility/noble class; author shorthand “noble/nobility” remains acceptable. |
| `aithren` — A-I-T-H-R-E-N | Individual participation/cultural identity; unchanged. |
| `vaelum` — V-A-E-L-U-M | Recognized persistent organization; unchanged. |

These are lowercase ordinary nouns. Existing `domai`, `aera`, `raeon`, `maege`, `maege glass`, `ru’ne`, `helkir`, `eln`, `ahm`, and the proper name `Nyx` retain their spelling and meaning. The package's straight-apostrophe spelling `ru'ne` is rendered as the established `ru’ne` in integrated canon; the original package bytes remain unchanged. “Element” continues to apply to Alchemical Type 1–5 materials.
