# Increment 001 — terminology and decision boundaries

Date: 2026-10-02. This addendum records explicit MK-147 decisions from Increment 001 and its [accompanying user request](../07_PROVENANCE/increment-001/EXPLICIT_UPDATE_REQUEST.txt). Read it with the [constitution](CONSTITUTION.md) and [v2 authority](V2_CANON_AUTHORITY.md).

## Terminology

`vaelum` is a lowercase ordinary noun, spelled V-A-E-L-U-M. `aithren` remains lowercase A-I-T-H-R-E-N: the individual/cultural participation identity. A vaelum is the persistent organization under which aithren may operate. These are distinct concepts.

Existing spellings remain: `domai`, `aera`, `raeon`, `maege`, `maege glass`, `ru’ne`, `helkir`, `eln`, `ahm`; `Nyx` is a proper name. Preserve original source spellings inside historical documents.

## Authority and accepted scope

Explicit Genesis decisions/corrections > later dedicated MK-147 canon > earlier recovery/workshop material > historical provenance. The increment adds explicit decisions without replacing unrelated v2 canon. The accepted scope is recorded in [vaelum and Accord Registry](../02_DOMAINS/VAELUM_AND_ACCORD.md) and [Hostile Organizations](../02_DOMAINS/HOSTILE_ORGANIZATIONS.md).

The Accord Registry registers and formally recognizes vaelum as a Crown administrative domain. Broader authority, travel systems, exact tax law, and additional governance machinery are not established by this update.
