## Core ontology
ELDRIS are stabilized persistent recursive-collapse structures produced by Genesis/chirality instability that becomes bounded by closure. Basin identity is not body pigment. Bodies are characteristically black/collapse-structured; basin identity leaks through markers/behavior.

## Basin combat behavior
- Red — physicality / contact melee: claws, teeth, body-force.
- Orange — contact melee plus energy intrusion; touch can create fatigue, destabilization, internal damage or healing-load pressure.
- Yellow — energy-mass projection: orbs, beams, rings, projected force. Introduces ranged danger, knockback/force, spacing, cover, shielding/barriers and line-of-sight discipline.
- Green — resonance / structural reinforcement; juggernaut persistence; larger/heavier reinforced melee structures; first major power jump.
- Blue — expressed energy projection; advanced Yellow-family ranged behavior. Projection can explode, burn, shear, detonate or create secondary effects after impact.
- Violet — high Genesis expression / mage-like manifestation; legendary/poorly understood territory.
