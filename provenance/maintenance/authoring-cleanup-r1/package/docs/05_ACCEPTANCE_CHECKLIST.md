# 05 — Acceptance criteria

## Required implemented outcome

1. The repository's active entry point routes to current canon and Arcs 1–7 without making checkpoint chronology the default workflow.
2. Topic pages contain reconciled substance and qualifications, not just one-line summaries or generic fantasy filler.
3. Seven real arc dossiers recover the already authored structure, identify factual owners, and preserve intentional blank time. Their chapter folders do not invent chapter counts or scenes.
4. Existing detailed sources, catalogs, visual references and historical packages remain accessible at their original paths.
5. An authority map tells a future agent where each topic's facts live. Coverage accounts for compatible inherited material and explicit supersessions.
6. Upstream inheritance and exclusion rules are explicit. Binding-circle exclusion does not erase MK157 Bindings. Mk-147's character plot is not merged into MK157.
7. Canon source revision, cleanup revision, macro closure, local OPENs and writing status are distinguished.
8. Every existing tracked file is unchanged except the two reviewed navigation files, with byte-exact Git backups of those originals. No tracked deletion/rename/mode change.
9. Existing active validators have before/after results and no new failures. Historical failures and blocked tests remain disclosed. No rewritten expected hashes or lowered checks.
10. Selected invariant checks, all new JSON, and all new/edited active links are validated. Full semantic review accompanies mechanical preservation.
11. The actual commit/branch/push state and remaining gaps are reported. No fabricated test execution or “fully recovered” claim for unreviewed sources.

## Concrete smoke tests for the authoring surface

A new reader should find, through at most a small number of intentional links:

- Kira's current biology, intrinsic Black identity, demonstrated kit and Orb knowledge limits.
- illi's current build, progression ownership and Project Princess Carry boundaries.
- Valnak's mechanism, social life, currency and ordinary versus core domai rewards.
- Where MK147 inheritance stops; where _raeon and _bricked are only relevant references.
- Each arc's entry and exit, authoritative chronology, dependencies, and unwritten chapter workspace.
- The exact current Trial extension and why a historical W36+ OPEN statement is not current law.
- The Arc Seven Trial sequence, final standing, gross amounts, remaining White bill, and why that is not a final bank balance.
- Where the old CP25 master survives and which schedule/accounting sections its correction superseded.
- Which data file owns a number and which CSV/JSON/Markdown files are validated mirrors.
- What still needs an author decision rather than an assistant's invention.

## Reject the cleanup if it does any of these

Deletes similar-looking history or mirrors; renames all files for aesthetics; puts the constitution behind a new conflicting instruction file; fills OPEN dates; moves combat to balance a ledger; promotes old Binding circles or character identity; changes price exceptions; regenerates catalogs; creates empty arc shells while claiming completion; or changes test expectations to conceal a data/source regression.

## Permitted honest partial outcome

If an independent section is safe to complete but a source gap or execution limitation prevents full coverage, retain the implemented verified portion and explicitly label the affected topics/arc sections unresolved. Do not lower the checklist to claim a full pass. A source recovery gap is not a retroactive reopening of the author's macro arc.
