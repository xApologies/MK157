# Red Week 3 / first-season Normal Raid boss development

R3D1 may pass off-page as ordinary recovery/social life.

## Normal Raid environment — WORKING
First-season Normal Raid can realize as a huge central cavern/nexus with six independent routes/domains. Route markers communicate boss rank.
Boss domains/environments are Valnak-designed and need not be ordinary eldris ecology. Standalone bosses remain independently attemptable.
Raid bosses are Genesis-fantasy/Transductive constructs/creatures, not ordinary eldris.

First-cycle roster remains **R/R/O/O/Y/G**.

## Red A — Fire Dragon
Large Valnak-designed dragon with fire-oriented Transduction. Fire/breath/surface combustion are physical+Genesis expressions, not generic magic.
Anatomy matters: wings/joints/tendons/sensory structures create meaningful melee targets. Guardian occupies decision space through proximity,
obstruction, injury and positioning, never an aggro statistic.

## Red B — Burrower
Large armored subterranean creature. Ground displacement telegraphs movement/emergence. Raid predicts emergence, creates attack windows and can
physically interfere with retreat. Core problem = positioning/windows.

## Orange A — Coherence Twins
Two smaller Raid-class giants/beasts. Each maintains a localized Coherence Field. When close enough for fields to overlap, the pair mutually
stabilize/restore damage. Solution is spatial separation, approximately splitting the ten-person raid 5+5. Losing separation allows recovery.
Core problem = two simultaneous fights + separation geometry.

## Orange B — Shardfield creature
Projects shard/anchor objects into the arena. Each establishes a bounded Decoherence/dissolution field. The boss progressively changes safe battlefield
geometry and tries to create enclosing topology around the group. Raid must reposition itself/boss and preserve escape/safe space.
No physical subdivision/add-spawning requirement.

## Yellow — Glasswing
Large flying crystalline/translucent-wing creature. While airborne it projects repeated ranged pressure across the raid and is intentionally difficult
for a conventional Guardian to occupy. Healer-heavy phase: Persistent/Directed Coherence and later Absorption are valuable. Raid disrupts/destroys
wing structures to force a crash/ground phase, after which Guardian/melee can meaningfully occupy it. Participants may bring legal equipment to cover
build weaknesses; exact tool OPEN.

## Green — Walking Fortress
Large quadrupedal juggernaut. Repeatedly maintains/reapplies an **Absorption Shield**. Encounter is a fatigue race: raid pressure must exceed shield
maintenance/recovery long enough to expose biological/structural vulnerabilities. Creature repeatedly charges through the arena.

Emergent strategy: split raid roughly 5+5 at opposite ends; use target concentration to induce back-and-forth charges; evade/reform; repeatedly focus
the same front limb during shield-break/turnaround windows. Crippling a front limb degrades charge geometry. This is player-discovered strategy,
not an aggro/taunt mechanic or Elara tutorial.

## Design principle
Raid difficulty should emerge from physical anatomy, Genesis architecture, behavior, environment, fatigue and coordinated geometry—not primarily
abstract inflated health.

## Red-season character reward handling
Payout rule remains once per participant per specific boss per season; distinct same-rank bosses each pay.
Current progression calendar books one Red first-clear and one Orange first-clear. If story later gives Kira+illi the additional Red and Orange first-clears,
each receives +6,250 wallet credits. Carry surplus into discretionary/social/card economy; do not move locked Binding dates automatically.
