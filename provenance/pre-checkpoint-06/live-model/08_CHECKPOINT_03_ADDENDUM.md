# Checkpoint 03 — Kira Battle Architecture and Biological Realization

This checkpoint is cumulative. Read after Checkpoints 01–02. Newer explicit canon supersedes conflicts; preserve all non-conflicting prior detail.

## Story progression
Arc One ends at the Champion's Table nightly highlights with Kira, illi, and Elara. Part Two begins with Kira and illi's friendship becoming an exceptional Duo Trial partnership.

Kira initially carries illi through deeper Duo waves. Deeper clears generate more credits; illi converts those credits into stronger sustainment; stronger sustainment lets Kira remain active longer; the Duo therefore recursively accelerates.

The seven-season middle-book rhythm alternates ordinary Valnak life, leisure/recovery, social events, shopping/Builder/Node use, and combat progression. Combat does not occupy every waking hour; residences and quiet downtime are major narrative spaces.

## Combat theaters
Four combat theaters:
1. Trials: Solo, Duo, Trio wave trials.
2. Dungeons: five-person Standard and Hard.
3. Raids: ten-person Boss Raid and Full Hard Raid.
4. Active Operations: military/formation-scale lowercase `domai`.

Prism remains sport, outside this combat-development hierarchy.

## domai
Normalize spelling to lowercase `domai`. Ranked Red through Violet; no White domai. They are bounded Genesis-collapse event landscapes with anomalously enlarged/non-isometric interiors and finite lifetimes. The structural center is a non-conscious superposed White Genesis Crystal; breaking it forces unraveling and creates an evacuation/harvest window.

Outside Valnak, a domai produces a basin-colored transient/aurora-like glow in the night sky. Inside Valnak, active domai are full-scale war-training environments.

The Node has an Active Operations map showing active domai, age, rank, rough footprint, estimated interior, core status, broad friendly/ELDRIS disposition, routes/fronts and unraveling state. Easy domai tend to be cleared earlier; difficult ones accumulate and drive larger community/military assaults.

## Node
The Node is Valnak's cognitive/spatial operating environment. It supports profile/ranks, friends, groups, messaging/voice, map/relocation, Combat queues, Shop/Auction House, Legacy progress, and Builder.

Builder allows hypothetical Binding configurations to be created, simulated, saved, shared, published and modified. Trending/most-viewed/highest-rated/Valnak-recommended builds exist. User-created builds are NOT called Legacies.

## illi White Legacy
After Kira and illi's first Duo success, illi is offered a historically extraordinary White Legacy. Elara has mischievously influenced the optimization environment, but illi legitimately qualifies.

Working White Legacy economy: ~70% of progression credits automatically service the Legacy; ~30% remain discretionary.

illi develops as a precision-sustainment/combat Helkir rather than a mass battlefield healer.

Signature first Binding: White Genesis absorption shield. It begins in White superposition and degrades:
White → Violet → Blue → Green → Yellow → Orange → Red → break.

This prevents injury rather than healing injury. In Support Solo, illi can repeatedly shield Valnak-generated combatants so they take essentially no biological damage until she fatigues. Her First-Cycle Support ranking therefore skyrockets; the mechanic is valid rather than patched.

Mature illi toolkit remains intentionally compact:
- White absorption/prevention;
- coherence-over-time;
- powerful targeted restoration;
- localized/personal barriers;
- modest offense/support.

## Kira Black design principle
Kira's Black Bindings provide freedom rather than highly automated packaged effects.

Progression principle:
access → control → fluency → integration.

Acquisition does not equal competency. Kira must learn to use each Binding through practice.

Kira's major Black systems are INTEGRATED SYSTEMS, not independent subsystems. At maturity they operate as one coherent Kira-state.

## Armor of the Abyss
Adaptive, intent-responsive battle body.
- extremely durable;
- absorbs/distributes large impacts;
- regenerates/re-resolves;
- cognitively responds to Kira without being separately conscious;
- reshapes within admissible body-linked geometry;
- Kira initially has five armored fingers and learns to resolve them into the characteristic three-claw combat morphology;
- her transformed hair can integrate into the armored state.

Armor itself is weapon and protection.

## CSR — Continuous Spatial Resolution
CSR gives continuous spatial position/orientation control, not merely generic flight.
- hover/suspension;
- three-dimensional translation;
- arbitrary orientation independent of gravity;
- rapid vector redirection/hypermobility;
- spatial leverage: Kira can brace against space and redistribute reaction forces without ordinary footing.

## Halo
Halo is Kira's Black-energy access and load-bearing energy architecture. It lets the Halo carry much of the sustained energetic "dumbbell" while Kira maintains the Halo.

Normal position: behind the crown/back of her head, slightly separated.
Working stages:
- Stage I: ~2 ft diameter; ordinary combat support; minimal fatigue.
- Stage II: ~4 ft diameter; much greater throughput; moderate fatigue.
- Stage III: ~6 ft diameter / ~3 ft radius; extreme battlefield-scale throughput; substantial accumulating fatigue.

Halo can carry energetic load for Armor, CSR, Genesis Orbs, Domain and shapings. It does not erase fatigue; cognitive load remains independently important.

Earlier arbitrary gold-energy Halo visual is superseded. Final Halo coloration must obey Genesis/Black visual grammar and remains OPEN.

## Genesis Orbs
Up to three persistent external Black Genesis-energy bodies, roughly cannonball-sized (~8 in / 20 cm diameter). They appear dense/solid.

They are mobile Transducers: Transductive events can originate through them. The Binding manifests/stabilizes/maintains them; Kira must learn what to do with them.

Early Kira mostly uses them as controllable cannonballs. Her signature learned technique is rapidly orbiting an Orb around herself as a moving disruption/exclusion geometry. It is not a force field; it physically collides with approaching ELDRIS and creates severe blunt trauma.

Control progression:
one Orb → orbit/fight with one → two → patterned/symmetric control → three → independent trajectories/transductions.

Genesis Orb practice teaches Kira to treat external structures as part of her controllable space and therefore prepares her for Domain extension.

## Domain
Domain is Kira's most general/potentially powerful Black Binding.

Natural state:
Domain = Kira herself.

It establishes an interactive resolution of the Genesis Field centered on Kira. It has no fixed purchased radius. Kira must learn to flex/push the boundary outward.

First activation gives overwhelming whole-body Genesis awareness: heart, circulation, lungs, tissues and integrated structures experienced simultaneously as one Genesis-field object.

Progression:
self → centimeters → room/local space → combat space → party → raid → battlefield.

Halo supplies energy to push/sustain Domain farther, but cannot substitute for control, filtering or knowledge. Stage III Halo eventually permits battlefield-scale Domain after extensive practice.

Domain mastery involves extent, resolution, bandwidth, control and attention/filtering. Information overload is a real limit.

Within Domain, Kira can potentially interact directly with Genesis-field structures. Crucial rule:
CAN MANIPULATE ≠ KNOWS HOW TO MANIPULATE.

If she learns the underlying field construction, Domain can potentially reproduce class-like effects without the ordinary packaged Binding:
- Helkir knowledge → healing/coherence shaping;
- maege knowledge → offensive Transduction;
- summoner knowledge → manifested entities;
- Architect knowledge → persistent/local field geometry;
- augmenter knowledge → self-modification.

Kira is not automatically all classes. Their disciplines remain bodies of specialized knowledge.

## Domain cognitive/battlefield interface
The battlefield HUD is an application of Domain.

Valnak participants already know the Node interaction grammar, so Kira can eventually extend a familiar interactive battlefield layer outside Valnak.

Working primitives:
Resolve → Link → Share → Mark → Query.

Link is consensual in normal use and preserves agency.

Linked participants contribute relevant observations to Domain. If one linked combatant sees an ELDRIS, its location can enter the shared tactical state and become available to other linked participants. Kira is not personally omniscient; the network performs distributed sensing.

Mature Domain creates a voluntary tactical hive/shared cognition without merging minds or removing free will. Kira acts like an orchestra conductor: shared targets, timing, corridors, defensive relationships, role synchronization and dynamic updates. Participants can accept, modify, decline and improvise.

Strategic command and Domain conduction remain distinct: a trained commander may decide WHAT must happen; Kira makes distributed execution coherent.

## Domain pinnacle
At extreme mastery Kira can cohere/decohere Genesis structures, induce local chirality instability and directly shape the Genesis Field inside Domain if she possesses sufficient knowledge/control.

Eventually battlefield ELDRIS cease to be the real problem: sufficiently mature Kira can eliminate them through Domain-scale Genesis interaction.

Her ultimate trajectory is to push Domain, supported by Stage III Halo, to the singularity-scale Genesis structure and stabilize the abnormal Yellow disequilibrium that drives Genesis collapses. The causal target is:
singularity disequilibrium → Genesis instability → collapse → domai → ELDRIS.

The endgame is therefore not "kill every monster" but correct the upstream Genesis condition so new collapses/monster threats stop.

## Kira post-Valnak biological realization — NEW CANON
Cannibalize ONLY the post-transformation biology from the recovered Enix Class 2 description. Ignore potion/Class-2 causality and old spell-system mechanics.

Kira enters Valnak Afflicted and is remade by Valnak's Black interaction into a Genesis-realized human state.

### Skin
- remains WHITE rather than becoming permanently black;
- unhealthy Afflicted chalk-white is replaced by extraordinarily healthy energized white;
- skin is physically thin but visually/energetically multilayered;
- conceptual analogue: multilayer optical behavior like octopus skin;
- observer has the impression of looking INTO the skin, not merely AT it;
- Kira's version has extreme apparent depth without obvious visual termination;
- this is Genesis resolution/depth, not physically infinitely thick tissue.

### Symmetry / beauty
Kira's post-transformation biological symmetry is effectively PERFECT.
She remains recognizably human but is pushed toward exotic/otherworldly beauty:
- extremely refined and symmetrical;
- angular/high cheekbones;
- refined jaw/craniofacial geometry;
- enlarged/restructured orbital sockets;
- slightly elongated/tapered ears;
- beautiful in an almost alien/otherworldly way without ceasing to read as human.

### Hair
- remains white;
- dramatically healthier, thicker and fuller;
- approximately 2–3× former apparent fullness as inherited working morphology;
- ordinary transformed biological hair, not arbitrary gold-energy hair.

### Eyes
Supersede the earlier gold Eye-of-Mordor design.
- black eyes / black internal depth;
- internal Red→Violet spectral/rainbow striation;
- visually echoes Black depth with trapped/resonating spectrum;
- enlarged orbital anatomy reinforces the alien/exotic effect;
- heightened perception of Genesis-energy/topological structure is part of the transformed visual system.

### Skeleton / nervous system / body
- skeleton is Genesis-integrated rather than merely denser;
- crystalline skeletal lamina/structure is retained as biological canon;
- nervous system and brain are systemically Genesis-integrated;
- hybrid crystalline-Genesis neural architecture is retained at concept level;
- musculature is integrated into whole-body Genesis realization;
- physique remains tall, lithe, dense, athletic and combat-efficient rather than bulky;
- long-legged / long reach characterization retained.
- MK157-specific height remains 5'8" pre-transformation → approximately 5'11" post-transformation.

Kira remains human, but baseline human biology has been pushed into a persistent coherent Genesis-integrated state.

### Visual contrast
Ordinary transformed Kira:
energized white deep skin + healthy white hair + black/rainbow eyes + exotic perfect symmetry.

Battle Kira:
that white Genesis-realized human state becomes overlaid/integrated with Black Armor of the Abyss, Black Genesis Orbs, Halo, CSR and Domain.

Black is therefore principally the battle/Genesis architecture rather than Kira's permanent skin pigmentation.
