# MK157 — VALNAK CITY / CULTURE / TRANSPORT CONTINUITY

This file isolates the city-facing material from Checkpoint 09 for easier Git discoverability.

## City character
Valnak is a utopian city/domain with ordinary civic life: residences, shops, restaurants, markets, entertainment, parks, waterfronts, institutions, sport, craft and leisure.

The book should introduce this through Kira/illi living in the city, not through lore dumps.

## Major mapped anchors
- PRISM — northeast sporting domain.
- CRAFTSMAN'S ROW — central Creator district.
- BLACKSTONE — southeast Institution of Knowledge.
- 001 — central `raeon` flagship + rooftop restaurant/Champion Tables.

## Residential author geography
Broad visible residential zones:

## Serialized map rule
If a location matters to the story, give it a compact serial marker on the map and a repository record.
Do not fully map every shop/street.
Serials identify places, not scenes.

## Local/macro transport
Macro region/domain movement: Node -> central hub.
Local movement: walk / appropriate personal movement / carriage.

## Valnak carriages
Autonomous enclosed rectangular-prism public vehicles unique to Valnak.
~12 passengers, captain chairs swivel, no conventional windows, interactive walls, one-way transparency available, privacy default common.
Hail -> enter -> choose destination -> physically travel -> exit.
No driver; no ordinary fare; managed municipal network.

## Central commercial district
The area around 001 is the author-facing equivalent of a prestigious flagship shopping corridor (Rodeo Drive / Fifth Avenue analog).
Do not require a canonical district name until the story needs one.

## Champion rooftop restaurant
Three stepped terraces; lower terrace reaches the edge.
Ten First-Cycle Solo Champion Tables at prime centered edge.
Ordinary seating uses reservations; food credit cost is trivial.
The skyline/sky display makes the venue iconic for nightly highlights.

## Kira table
Kira stays #1 First-Cycle Solo all cycle.
Center table is effectively always hers.
Her uninterrupted reign produces the cultural title "Eternal Champion."

## Social visibility
Kira/illi/Elara become fixtures of the highlight venue when they choose to attend.
They are not obligated to appear every night.
