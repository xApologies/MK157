# MK157 — PRISM WORLD-CLOCK TRACKING MODEL

## World population
There may be hundreds or more teams across Valnak instances.

The author model explicitly tracks **37**.

Tracked does not mean championship contender.

## Why 37
The teams provide a persistent background population.
Kira can meet a member of Team 32 in Red season, ignore the team for months, then recognize the same team if it later climbs into contention.

## Standings
Regular-season standings use:
**Wins / Losses**

No season-long elimination bracket.

Approximately 20 teams occupy the meaningful moving competitive/playoff bubble at a given time.

Top 16 qualify for postseason.

Postseason:
16 -> 8 -> 4 -> 2 -> Champion

Team names, rosters, team sizes, positions, exact sport rules, match frequency, tie-breaks, seeding and playoff-week placement remain OPEN.

## Credits
Prism participants can earn Valnak credits through validated performance and team success.
Exact formula remains OPEN.
Do not reduce all contribution to goals/disables; mobility, control, defense and role-specific contribution can matter.

## Valnak map
PRISM northeast district is a configurable large sporting territory.
Approx ~7-mile footprint on current map.
Can partition into multiple large play domains or combine area for major matches.
Working ordinary field examples: ~1.5x2, 2x2, 3x3 miles.
Three-dimensional Transductionist mobility is expected.
