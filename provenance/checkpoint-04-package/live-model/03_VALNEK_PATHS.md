# Valnek, Elara, Paths, automation, residences, cities

## Valnek / Elara

Valnek is a **conscious domain of existence**, not merely a city
containing an AI. It can evaluate provenance, grant credits, restructure
people, instantiate trials, restore participants, maintain
residences/cities, generate Paths, and learn.

**Elara** is Valnek's personified social manifestation and **Custodian
of Valnek**. She is not a separate goddess/daughter/ruler. Most people
interact with Valnek through the environment/node; direct Elara
interaction is rare/intimate and socially prestigious.

## Developer/runtime model

Elara is like a developer working inside her own environment.

Between 23-year cycles she reviews results and develops/configures
Valnek: Paths, incentives, interfaces, training, services, validated
discoveries.

During an active cycle most operation is automated runtime. Elara does
not manually administer billions. She can intervene with privileged live
access. Automation can produce results she dislikes while still obeying
rules she authored.

Loop: observe civilization → model people/needs → configure Valnek →
cycle launches → automated Paths/trials/economy → human experimentation
→ provenance/results → cycle closes → Elara reviews/develops → next
release.

Thus Valnek can "surprise" Elara without being a separate mind.

## Paths

A Path is a **Valnek-validated developmental trajectory**, not merely a
discount bundle.

Generation weights: - demonstrated aptitude, - biology/current state, -
history, - intention, - affiliations/context, - current build, -
civilizational need.

Free will remains. A person may refuse/diverge, but Valnek makes useful
trajectories economically attractive.

The discount is incentive; the deeper product is a validated route of
continued development that reduces build stagnation. Paths have
checkpoints and can branch/combine after completion.

Valnek continually updates Paths to train civilization against ELDRIS
and occupy individual competency. Human discoveries can be validated and
incorporated into future catalogs/Paths; humanity teaches Valnek while
Valnek teaches humanity.

## Path tiers / packages

Normal historical Paths: Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet.
White is legendary/mythic, perhaps appearing only on century-scale
rarity; society knows White Paths from legends.

Working package concept, exact percentages OPEN: - Red/Orange: strong
discount on \~one ability. - Yellow/Green: package/discount around
\~two. - Blue/Violet: package/discount around \~three. - White:
extraordinary curated trajectory with massive subsidy/importance.

Earlier brainstorm examples included low-tier 20--25% style discounts
and higher package subsidies up to 60--80%; do not lock percentages yet.

## Black Path

Kira produces an unprecedented classification. **Black is not Rank 8.**
The historical human Path vocabulary ends at White. Valnek's generator
encounters Kira and creates/expresses a category it has never needed.

Kira sees **six Black plaques/options**. She initially has no cultural
framework for them and can simply ask what they are. Elara understands
how impossible the event is because she built Valnek.

Black options are Valnek trying to instantiate known human Transductive
roles against Kira's new Black-compatible biology. Current initial
set: 1. Black maege --- Black offensive/ranged shaping concept; exact
mature ability OPEN. 2. Black summoner --- Black-compatible summon;
exact entity/mechanics OPEN. 3. Black augmenter --- Black-compatible
self-augmentation; exact mature behavior OPEN. 4. Black architect ---
localized Genesis-field/velis-like topology/control; exact mature
behavior OPEN. 5. Black healer --- coherence/decoherence architecture
capable of sustainment and potentially offensive destabilization; exact
mature behavior OPEN. 6. **Armor of the Abyss** --- Kira-specific armor
architecture combining Black compatibility with her armorsmith identity.

Older placeholder names Blackstar/Nightwyrm/Overstate/Requiem/Blackweave
may remain provenance-only until deliberately reassigned; do not
silently force them onto the new six.

## Ability wall / credits

First-time combat entrants normally confront an enormous wall of
available entry-level shapings, effectively
horizon-to-horizon/floor-to-ceiling possibilities. They have roughly
**1000 starting credits**.

Example ordinary build: 300-credit fireball + 700-credit personal
shield. Exact prices are illustrative.

Kira does not get the ordinary wall. Valnek's search space initially
tries to explode outward because normal pruning fails, producing the
comedy of Elara fighting her own runtime. Elara/Valnek collapses the
presentation to six Black options, each effectively consuming the full
1000-credit entry budget while representing an absurd/mythic subsidy.

## Plaque visual language

Canonical working display: - simple rectangular plaque; - border is
living light in rank color; - no ornate gem/diamond UI furniture; -
interior stays dark/readable for ability information; - visual energy
inside is subtle, roughly \~10% opacity, to give depth; -
R/O/Y/G/B/V/White have corresponding luminous borders; - Black is
exceptional: abyssal black with a **liquid neon rainbow trapped deep
inside**, evolving geometry intermittently visible; - Black should read
abyss first, neon second.

Selected visual reference is packaged.

## Residences / city instancing

After the initial trial, every participant wakes in a personal
**permanent residence**. It persists across future 23-year cycles.

Residences can be cheaply customized: forest/stream, island/beach,
apartment, etc. Residence door is an addressable transition into public
Valnek.

Valnek public cities are instantiated/compartmentalized copies to
prevent billions of people occupying one physical city. Each instance
can share the same shops/restaurants/services/layout. People have a
default city instance; map selection/routing can move them where
allowed.

Grouping through the HUD/node allows friends from different instances to
form a group and be routed into a shared instance. Valnek may spin up a
new instance for occupancy/grouping. Exact graph/VM math is author-side
and not reader-essential.

First-cycle participants are cohort-gated: they interact/group primarily
with other first-cycle participants so ancient Violet/White veterans
cannot power-level them through content. Later-cycle population has
broader access.

## City visual constitution

Valnek city is utopian/fantasy-futurist, not an Earth road-grid city: -
glass and metal, - integrated vegetation, - gardens/trees in
architecture, - running water, pools, streams, waterfalls, - large open
plazas, - airy vertical structures, - clean luminous advanced design, -
nature and architecture interwoven.

Exact mileage, hubs, occupancy caps, and final map remain OPEN.
