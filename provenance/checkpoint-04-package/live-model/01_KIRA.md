# Kira --- Affliction, evolution, transformation, design

## Before Valnek

Kira is 22 and reaches Valnek genuinely **Afflicted**. Earlier same-day
material saying she was only mistaken for Afflicted is superseded.

Working evolutionary model: Genesis-collapse/Affliction conditions have
exerted biological pressure over generations. Many mutations/variants
never stabilize. Kira is the first known Afflicted whose relevant
biological/genetic change crosses a viable threshold. She is still
pathological on entry; evolution has not yet separated the useful
phenotype from Affliction. Her changed biology permits unprecedented
Black compatibility. She is a transitional first successful phenotype,
not necessarily the final evolutionary form.

This does not grant knowledge, skill, or perfect control.

## Blacksmith identity

Kira was raised in an armorsmith household and wants the
Creation/Craftsman route. Her specialty is mail. From childhood she
learns wire preparation, mandrel/coiling, clipping rings, closures,
weaving, repairs, garments, fit and articulation. At 22 she is an
exceptional mail armorsmith: fast, precise, low-waste, highly dexterous,
excellent at seamless repairs and fitting. She loves many links becoming
one protective surface.

Smithy work gives functional strength, endurance, strong
forearms/back/legs, dexterity, and familiarity moving ingots, tools,
armor and stock.

## What Valnek sees

Valnek first recognizes Afflicted, then detects something unprecedented.
Elara perceives it through being Valnek. The normal
compatibility-pruning system does not find the expected ceiling. Elara
tries to persuade Kira to try Combat rather than immediately choosing
Craftsman.

Kira is presented with six unprecedented Black offerings; **Armor of the
Abyss** attracts her because she is an armorsmith.

## Brownout transformation

Valnek attempts the Black Binding/chirality restructuring. Normally a
bounded restructuring reaches saturation. Kira does not.

The operation opens her into Black/Abyss. She begins accepting/drawing
energy beyond Valnek's expected load. Power-supply analogy: Valnek may
have a 1000-watt available throughput while Kira behaves like a
2000-watt demand.

She does not empty the Abyss. She drives **Valnek's available
draw/throughput** to its ceiling. Valnek visibly dims/browns out, like
lights during a grid disturbance. Elara performs an emergency override
and cuts the process.

Before cutoff, Kira is comprehensively remade: R--V-organized human
realization → Black organization.

The Afflicted pathology is gone afterward. Kira remains human, but her
stable body is now a Genesis-energy realization pushing the bounds of
human materiality.

## Canonical post-transformation design

Height: 5'8" → **5'11"**. She notices changed eye line, stride, reach,
balance and proprioception. Arms/legs are proportionally longer.

Build: **thin, lithe, trim, muscular, unusually dense**. Natural
fighter's physique. Not bulky/bodybuilder-heavy and not willowy.

Skin: extremely deep **matte black**, smooth and ultra-fine with an
almost chalk-like optical texture. Depth without gloss. Not metallic,
obsidian, rough or dusty.

Nails: even blacker than skin and completely non-reflective---quiet
optical-void black.

Eyes: most striking feature. Narrow/deep vertical Black pupil surrounded
by turbulent radiant **golden Genesis energy**. Visual inspiration:
Eye-of-Mordor-like vertical geometry converted from fire to golden
energy. Alien-looking but Kira remains human.

Hair: long, made of fine individual strands/filaments of **golden
Genesis energy**. It still reads and moves as hair. Energy shimmers
along strands. Not cables, tethers, tentacles or literal flames.

Face/skeleton: recognizably Kira but more angular/refined: high
cheekbones, clean angular jaw, sharper facial planes, slightly larger
orbital sockets/eye presence, exotic high-fashion-model-like geometry
without exaggeration.

Ears: slightly longer/narrower with subtle taper; still human, not full
elf ears.

## Armor of the Abyss

Armor is not equipment worn over Kira. Since she is remade in Genesis
energy, armor manifestation is a **reconfiguration of her own
energetic/chirality structure**.

Her long golden hair ceases being organized as hair and resolves into
the armor. The armor can close seamlessly over head/body. When
dismissed, energy re-resolves into Kira's ordinary post-transformation
form.

Visual: seamless body-conformal liquid Black; abyss/deep-water depth;
unified neon R--V rainbow/geometry appears trapped far beneath the
surface and only intermittently manifests. Black dominates; neon reveals
structure.

Initial hand geometry: three large claws per hand---thumb; index+middle;
ring+pinky. Biological fingers remain; armor forms the larger claw
geometry.

Initial combat vocabulary remains small: armor + claws. Extraordinary
access does not equal mature skill/control/output.
