# Thread constitution additions

## Incidental Trial / lifestyle-credit doctrine
Locked calendars govern major progression, required combat, recovery blocks, and milestone credit pipelines. They are NOT exhaustive activity logs.

Participants may run short/shallow paid Trials during otherwise available personal time and voluntarily stop between waves under existing exit rules. Off-page runs may generate modest incidental credits for food, abecca, inexpensive clothes/dresses, residence customization/plants, entertainment, gifts, and similar lifestyle spending.

These runs do not silently replace/move required calendar events, alter major standings, or move milestone purchase dates. They need not be individually narrated/timestamped. Later Kira may also run shallow Red/Orange/Yellow portions as exercise/stress relief without committing to maximum multi-day depth.

The formal illi progression ledger is a modeled progression pipeline, not her literal every-credit bank statement.

## Narrative-time doctrine
Calendar = structurally significant macro time. Novel = narratively significant time. Do not simulate every day/hour. OPEN days may pass off-page.

## Noble naming morphology
Noble/royal families preserve a conservative gendered orthographic convention:
- masculine personal names strongly favor `ae`;
- feminine personal names strongly favor `ai`.
This is class-coded naming culture, not universal language law. Common/non-noble families need not follow it; Kira is an example.
`illi` is a deliberate royal-family exception chosen by her mother and remains lowercase. Do not normalize `illi` into the ae/ai pattern.

## Lexical reinforcement
- `illi` lowercase; Ellie/Illy/Ily/Ilya -> `illi` when context is clear. No separate Ellie.
- Elara, not Alara.
- Valnak, not Valmek/Valmec/Valneck/etc.
- `raeon`, not Rayon.
- `abecca` = coffee-equivalent; conversational coffee maps to abecca.
- `velis` canonical.
