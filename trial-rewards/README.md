# Trial rewards — Checkpoint 18

[CSV](TRIAL_WAVE_CREDITS.csv) and [JSON](TRIAL_WAVE_CREDITS.json) are exact supplied W1–35 rows. Cumulative payout is **26,955** per eligible participant. Every completed wave pays its row; a failed next wave pays nothing. Solo/Duo/Trio share the schedule and each eligible participant receives the full amount, without dividing by party size. Legitimate repeat runs pay again. W36+ payout remains OPEN.

Checkpoint 18 halves each immediately prior locked wave payout using HALF_UP. The old 53,910 and earlier ~55,000 rounded target are superseded numeric values; 55,030 was an arithmetic error. Rejected high-output tables remain non-authoritative. Forty-nine hypothetical complete runs yield **1,320,795** credits by arithmetic, not a new story schedule. Existing ~156-hour timing remains an author planning estimate.

The prior economic design remains: early waves pay useful but modest amounts, with shallow reward growth relative to Binding rank costs. An Exceptional B=17,000 Binding's White rank alone costs 3,084,480; the hypothetical 49-run total funds about 0.43 such purchases before other spending. White mastery remains a career/multi-cycle capital project. Veterans can have broad lower/mid-rank architecture but only a few White Bindings; Legacy discounts and prerequisite compression materially change lifetime economics. Credits compete with Auctions, equipment, materials, cards and leisure.
