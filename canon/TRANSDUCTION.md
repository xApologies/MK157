# Transduction, Creation and Builder


## Architecture and energy

The working operational chain is stored Binding architecture → phase-state shadow projection (PSSP) → basin access/energy → energized field topology → concept → conscious collapse → shaping. A Binding is not its active shaping. Better concept improves realization, efficiency, stability/control and energy cost. Veteran efficiency comes from clean projection, low waste, sequencing, release discipline, basin discipline and judgment, not using a “lower-color response.” [Operational chain](../prior-checkpoint-source/LIVE_MODEL_03_SYSTEMS_STORY.md#transduction-core); [PSSP and color](../live-model/02_COSMOLOGY.md#4-color-culture--basin-firewall).

The six domains are Persistent Topology/Coherence–Decoherence, Dimensional Geometry, Genesis Field Topology, Genesis Expression, Resonance and Chirality. R/O/Y/G/B/V are structural energy identities; topology determines the required combination. They are not elements, classes, domains or universal power levels. Ordinary Binding development separately uses R→W: Red–Violet deepens the primary mechanism; White supplies a mechanism-derived secondary expression. Professions/build affinities are metadata. [Domains](../THREAD_DEVELOPMENT_CONSTITUTION.md#six-transduction-domains); [Basin firewall](../bindings/CANON_LOCKS.md#absolute-energy-basin-rule); [Rank development](../THREAD_DEVELOPMENT_CONSTITUTION.md#binding-economy).

There is no mana system. Fatigue is loss of sustainable physiological/endurance-like access; a basin still exists when a person cannot maintain access. If a required basin exhausts, every architecture needing it becomes unavailable until recovery. Training improves efficiency/endurance. Do not substitute cooldown clocks, taunts, threat tables, aggro statistics or gear score for physical/biological/informational/Genesis mechanisms. [Fatigue](../THREAD_DEVELOPMENT_CONSTITUTION.md#fatigue); [Later mechanism clarification](../live-model/SUPERSESSIONS.md#checkpoint-12-complete-pricing).

## Professions, healing and manifestations

The exact three Coherence primitives are Directed Coherence, Persistent Coherence and Coherence Field, mirrored by three Decoherence operations. Injury type does not create new heal Bindings. Expanded Coherence Diagnostic is diagnostic rather than another healing primitive. Absorption prevents injury. helkir is the profession; combat medic is an ALPHA role label. Augmenters are Resonance-led, with limited natural overlap. Architects work directly with topology, manifolds, knots, constraints, boundaries, anchors/velis and construction rather than a padded combat-spell list. Chirality is organizational grammar/admissibility; high-end dismantling concerns persistent structure, not arbitrary color magic. [Healing lock](../bindings/CANON_LOCKS.md#coherence--decoherence-lock); [Architect](../bindings/CANON_LOCKS.md#architect); [Chirality](../bindings/CANON_LOCKS.md#chirality).

Summons commonly combine Genesis Expression, Resonance and Chirality. They are non-sentient but autonomous within validated command/behavior packages; low loads can last hours while extreme output can exhaust required access in seconds/minutes. Ranks develop one manifestation rather than making seven entities. The 229 profiles include 180 elemental manifestations, 40 other creature patterns and nine Primes. Candidate ALPHA status remains separate from locked price status. [Entity catalog scope](../summons/README.md).

Primes are specialist humanoid/person-like autonomous manifestations, useful even at Red, with load-dependent endurance and growing efficiency/coherence/autonomy. Genesis is offensive, Coherence restorative, Resonance frontline/persistence; the wider taxonomy includes Dimensional, Chirality, Combustion, Lightning, Mineral and Atmospheric. Genesis Field Prime was not adopted. PE-001–003 ordinary graphs require all listed prerequisites at White; illi's specific Green→Red gates compress access without granting the omitted tools. PE-004–009 grounded recipe selections remain ALPHA/WORKING. [Taxonomy](../live-model/PRIME_ELEMENTALS.md#12-prime-elementals); [Exact ordinary graphs](../live-model/PRIME_ELEMENTALS.md#locked-ordinary-recipes-for-illis-three-primes--all-listed-prerequisites-white); [Candidate recipes](../live-model/PRIME_ELEMENTALS.md#integrated-graphs).

Hydrogen Orb and Premixed Combustible Gas Orb are the two narrow gas concepts. Hydrogen needs appropriate oxidizer/mixing/ignition; pressure alone does not canonically ignite it. Do not proliferate separate Air/Pressure/Shock/Cold Bindings for emergent uses. Costs follow the current price matrix; rank expressions, White development and basin topology remain OPEN. [Gas mechanism](../live-model/15_CHECKPOINT_08_LIVE_MODEL_DELTA.md#13-new-gas-bindings--atmospheric-prime-seed); [Current price scope](../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#semantics-and-protected-fields).

## Creation, material knowledge and cultural work

Creation is a primary route, not combat-class identity. Craft, ru’ne/sigil work and Alchemy integrate actual material knowledge with Transduction. The evaluation pattern reproduce→repair→construct→adapt→solve→create is an author model, not a color assessment table. Difficulty, quality, contribution and innovation govern evaluation rather than hours or redundant output. Ordinary material→craft→evaluation→credits→recycling sustains practice; exceptional commissions, original art/design and Auction pieces may be retained. Valnak's ability to reproduce a design does not erase the premium of original provenance. No second currency or ten-thousand-identical-swords income farm follows. [Complete Creator owner](../live-model/CREATOR_CRAFTSMAN.md).

Craftsman's Row supports masters, students, clients, demonstrations and social life. Blacksmiths' Row's four sides × two tiers × eight smithies = 64 is a WORKING premium-address model, not every smithy in Valnak. Persistent shops are property rather than temporary stalls; succession, transfer, death/retirement and inter-instance semantics remain OPEN. A smithy is the workplace and a forge one component. Clean controlled open work can suit spectators. Matter conduction requires metallurgy, heat, geometry, joining and stress knowledge; it is not generic metal telekinesis, tools remain useful, and specialization matters. Kira's manual mail expertise and continuing Creation aspiration survive her Combat route. [Craft detail and qualifications](../live-model/CREATOR_CRAFTSMAN.md).

ru’ne marks and operational sigils concern Genesis structures; an illustrative seven-harvest crop is WORKING, not a universal production rate. Architects' field topology is related but distinct. Alchemy is material science; naturally occurring vaen are unusual materials/resources, not personal Binding sources. [Creation inheritance](../prior-checkpoint-source/LIVE_MODEL_03_SYSTEMS_STORY.md#creation).

## Builder and authority limits

The existing 200 candidate Legacy trajectories remain infrastructure, not Kira/illi canon. Node Builder supports browse/search, architecture inspection, hypothetical builds, multiple saves, publishing/sharing and lightweight reactions, with Featured/New/Trending/recommended surfaces. No compulsory strategy essays, forums or comment mechanics. Veterans' retrospective builds and first-cycle budget theorycraft coexist. Elara publishes and cares about reception without automatic correctness. Parties involve food/cards/argument/jokes and sometimes later unpaid Training Yard testing; repeated Dungeon acquaintances become overlapping friendship circles. Y5D3 invitation and Y5D5 first attendance are locked; the inviter stays unnamed. [Builder community](../builder/COMMUNITY.md).

Use the [data index](../data/INDEX.md) for exact registries, mirrors, recipes and validator routes. Pricing finality never promotes ALPHA mechanisms, generated candidates or unresolved basins into authored character facts. No catalog regeneration is part of this maintenance layer.
