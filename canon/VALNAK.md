# Valnak


## Conscious domain and civilization

Valnak evaluates provenance, grants credits, restructures participants, instantiates training, restores participants, maintains residences/cities, generates Legacies and learns. Elara is its personified Custodian. This is civilization's survival/development engine: Combat protects civilization and Creation develops it. Education, tourism, shopping, visits to permanent residents and ordinary life are valid participation. Returning people may spend carried credits and leave after weeks. [Domain identity](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#valnak--elara); [Persistent participation](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#8-node-credential--persistent-dossier); [Creation](../live-model/CREATOR_CRAFTSMAN.md).

Permanent Valnak uses continuous local Genesis/starscape throughput. Approximately 23 years of accumulated surplus sustains the much larger seasonal deployment; the permanent domain continues afterward. Color seasons are not a basin-consumption schedule. Valnak is planet-localized; the planet's proper name and exact architecture remain OPEN. [Energy and localization](../live-model/02_COSMOLOGY.md#9-permanent-vs-seasonal-valnak-energy).

Families, militaries, professions and veterans prepare builds, groups and equipment before gates open. Kira's parents have prior experience; she knows the institution. Elara can revise panels, landscaping and city implementation between cycles without making Valnak culturally unknown. [Familiarity owner](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#familiarity-across-cycles).

## Legacies, entry and the sandbox

A Legacy is a validated developmental trajectory weighted by aptitude, biology, history, intention, affiliations/context, current build and civilizational need. People can refuse/diverge; economic incentives are not conscription. Checkpoints and later combinations support development, and human experimentation feeds future Valnak knowledge. Red–Violet ordinary tiers and legendary White describe offers/architecture, not immediate capability ranks. The rough one/two/three-ability packages and generic discount examples remain WORKING; illi's accepted package has its own explicit 55% rule. [Trajectory](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#legacies); [Generic tier qualifications](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#legacy-tiers--packages); [Actual pricing](../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#entry-and-white-legacy).

First entry grants 2,000 credits. One Foundational Red Binding costs 1,700; Kira's approximately 1,000 Armor price is a distinct anomaly. White Legacy compresses prerequisites/recipes/access and economics but does not confer omitted prerequisite Bindings or automatically White-rank new abilities. Black is Kira's unprecedented category, not Rank 8. [Grant and exceptions](../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#entry-and-white-legacy); [Compression](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#3-white-legacy).

First-cycle restrictions cover progression-bearing grouping with mature veterans, not social or geographical quarantine. Reaching Green Solo is sufficient for same-cycle graduation; clearing the whole Green band is unnecessary. Kira receives no exemption. Before illi's independent B1D1 W13 clear, Kira voluntarily remains centered on first-cycle friends. Afterward both may group with veterans, but dossiers, reputation, experience and build fit still affect invitations. [Restriction](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#5-first-cycle-sandbox--green-graduation); [Eligibility versus credibility](../live-model/31_CHECKPOINT_24_ARC6_GRADUATION.md#4-graduation-consequence).

## Residence, Node and movement

Personal residences persist across cycles and can be cheaply customized. Doors are addressable transitions into public Valnak. Instanced cities can share layout and services, with default instances and group routing managing population. Exact graph/VM/occupancy mathematics is OPEN. A persistent Node dossier records identity, credits, property, Bindings/ranks, Legacy, standings and qualifications. The passive anchored credential is infrastructure, not a bought/ranked build capability. Outside, a trivial shaping projects a compact read-only authenticated credential: Valnak provides facts and institutions determine policy. Synchronization after closure remains OPEN. [Residences](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#residences--city-instancing); [Dossier](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#8-node-credential--persistent-dossier).

Macro movement uses Node→hub; local movement uses walking, personal movement or public carriage. Carriages physically travel: enclosed autonomous rectangular vehicles around 12 passengers, swiveling captain chairs, interactive walls, optional one-way transparency and common privacy, no driver or ordinary fare. They are Valnak-specific; propulsion remains unassigned. City design blends glass/metal, plants, water, plazas and airy architecture. [Transport](../live-model/VALNAK_CITY_CULTURE_TRANSPORT.md#localmacro-transport); [Carriages](../live-model/VALNAK_CITY_CULTURE_TRANSPORT.md#valnak-carriages).

Participant embodiment is a PSSP tied to maintained authoritative outside body/state. Injury is lived pain, blood, fear and loss of proprioception; projection destruction does not destroy that outside state. Kira re-instances safely on a residence bed and returns gently over roughly 15 experiential seconds, a WORKING narrative target rather than a hard timer. Memory remains without peak-combat panic. Some entrants reject further combat and choose other Valnak life. [Embodiment owner](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#pssp-embodiment-death-and-re-instancing).

Elara personally supplies Kira's brief welcome and Node grammar: summon/dismiss, navigation, selection, apparent touch or thought. Existing categories are dossier/identity/credits, build, standings/qualifications, travel, social and Builder. This introduces operation rather than inventing extra UI rules. [Primer](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#residence-node-primer).

## Clock and ordinary life

The Valnak coordinate is season/week/day: seven R→W seasons × seven weeks × seven days = 343 days, each 31 hours. The planetary year/month discrepancy and external subjective-time conversion are separate OPEN questions. Red–Violet use pleasant pastel skies; White the illuminated-world dark starscape. [Valnak calendar](../live-model/02_COSMOLOGY.md#checkpoint-11-calendar-clarification); [Seasonal presentation](../THREAD_DEVELOPMENT_CONSTITUTION.md#valnak).

World, Kira and illi have synchronized but independent clocks. Highlights run at 25:00 daily; attendance is voluntary. Each season has raeon W5 qualification/opening, W6 progression, W7D1–D4 late bracket, W7D5 championship and Auction W7D5–D7. Prism runs Red W1–Violet W7, then White W1–2 round of 16, W3 quarters, W4 semis, W5 buildup, W6 championship and W7 closing. Author calendars are sparse; an OPEN/home/recovery day is meaningful, not an automatic farm slot. [Standing clock](../live-model/WORLD_CLOCK.md#nightly-highlight-reels); [Lived rhythm](../live-model/SOCIAL_LIFE_AND_FOUNDATIONS.md#abecca-and-ordinary-days).

Kira's table, Builder friends, abecca, two card flagships, crafts, sport and foundations are part of lived civilization. The [world/culture view](WORLD_AND_CULTURE.md) retains institutional detail. The [seven arc dossiers](../story/INDEX.md) constrain selected events without converting every clock row into a chapter. Older Day-One Solo/Day-Two double-Duo timestamps yield to the CP18 combat scaffold; story beats survive with displaced timing OPEN.


## Arc One R2 residence direction

Grouped wipe/death recovery may designate a host residence with temporary private guest recovery rooms; this is WORKING, not permanent expansion, and successful exits need not re-instance. Kira's lush volcanic-island residence becomes the girls' base; exact layout, price and room count remain OPEN. Ordinary modules are comparatively cheap mature infrastructure. The royal estate houses only the King, Queen and six children when present, with Valnak-bound attendants; Crown staff have separate residences.
