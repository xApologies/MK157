# Combat and encounter ecology


## Trials, standings and delivery

Solo/Duo/Trio share a fixed 3×3-mile, 9-mi² arena. Central Basin supplies maneuvering and visibility with exposure; Pillars supply cover/funnels but conceal threats; Lake/Wetlands/stream creates crossings and movement constraints; Open Rise supplies observation/range but little cover; Broken Ground supplies concealment/chokepoints at navigation and awareness cost. Geography blends naturally rather than five perfectly partitioned biomes. Map labels are author annotations. [Terrain](../live-model/TRIAL_ARENA.md#5-trial-arena-geography); [Approved map scope](../live-model/TRIAL_ARENA.md#12-visual-canon).

W1 delivers one eldris, later waves follow the prime-number population model; totals delivered are not simultaneous population. Perimeter ingress responds to throughput, so fast kills bring replacement rather than empty waiting. R W1–3 / O W4–7 / Y W8–12 / G W13–18 / B W19–25 / V W26–34 / W W35+ define standing bands. White W35+ raw count is capped; configuration/organization changes, with the exact cap and spawn law OPEN. The older W35 pressure-saturation hypothesis is WORKING. [Thresholds and timing](../live-model/COMBAT_THRESHOLDS.md); [Current White count and reward lock](../live-model/32_CHECKPOINT_25_ARC7_FINALE.md#trial-w36--new-lock).

Normal paid runs start W1. Every eligible member receives the full cumulative payout, including legitimate repeats; failed next waves pay zero and party size does not divide the reward. W1–35 totals 26,955; every completed W36+ pays 1,600, so C(N)=26,955+1,600×(N−35), N≥35. W96 pays 124,555 and W100 130,955 each. Old 53,910/~55,000 and W36+ OPEN statements are superseded. [Reward owner](../trial-rewards/README.md#checkpoint-25-w36-lock).

Voluntary termination happens during the brief inter-wave break, never during committed wave combat. Training Yard allows a prior completed/unlocked wave start for the relevant formation and pays no Trial credits; other official standing/unlock effects remain OPEN. Kira's low-wave paid practice is deliberate learning and fallback, not lost standing. [Exit](../live-model/TRAINING_YARD.md#trial-exit-rule); [Practice](../live-model/TRAINING_YARD.md#training-yard).

Narrative bands per wave are R20–40 min, O45–75 min, Y1–2 h, G2–3.5 h, B3.5–6 h, V6–12 h, White 12+h/extreme multi-day. Violet W26≈6 h, W30≈9 h, W34≈12 h are anchors. W1–18 is about 21–38 h/29.5 central; W1–35 central≈156 h=5 days+1 h at 31 h/day. Kira's mature ≈4-day estimate remains WORKING. These are not stopwatch guarantees or constant kill-time multiplication. Multi-day pushes, fatigue and recovery matter. CP25's ordered White W1 program governs without inventing exact intraday timings or shorter universal durations. [Timing model](../live-model/COMBAT_THRESHOLDS.md); [Finale scheduling](../world-clock/ARC7_REVISED_DIRECTOR_CALENDAR.md).

Counts remain OPEN; the established working tiebreak direction is not promoted into an invented full scoring system. Armor + claws, Red melee/Orange intrusion/Yellow ranged geometry and saturation govern the first fight, with no early CSR/Orbs/Halo/Domain. ## eldris and reusable composition

eldris are stabilized persistent recursive-collapse structures: energy-mass closure sustains recursion rather than ordinary flesh. Black wispy/rootlike/smoky/branching/fractured bodies leak basin identity through eyes, wounds, traces, impact and behavior. Red contact, Orange intrusion, Yellow projected force, Green reinforcement/juggernaut persistence, Blue advanced expressed projection and Violet high Genesis expression are behavior families. A=R/O/Y, B=G/B, C=V is qualitative threat grouping, not a damage formula, universal domain mapping or species/class table. S3 is structural intuition and the spirit analogy grants no new spirit system. [Ontology and behavior](../live-model/ELDRIS_REFERENCE.md).

The 209 atomic groups enumerate unordered basin multisets of 1–4 bodies with a highest R/O/Y/G/B/V ceiling. Author IDs are not in-world names. Higher permitted content can combine/duplicate all legal lower groups; R/O/G melee and Y/B/V Transductionist are composition metadata. The documented example 2×R004+2×O006+Y012+G018+B021 totals 25 bodies, not approximately 30. There are no White eldris. Registry availability does not itself authorize content types. [Atomic rules](../builder/encounters/eldris/BUILDER_CONTRACT.md#hard-constraints); [Composition example](../builder/encounters/eldris/BUILDER_CONTRACT.md#example).

## Dungeons and Raids

Domain ≠ rank ≠ generated realization. Valnak Normal and Hard Dungeons span R→W, with cumulative R→V eldris and the same 209 groups. Their pre-populated populations roam, hunt, separate, react and converge; this is not Trial inflow. No rubber-banding punishes over-ranked veterans. Whole-build integration/competence/fatigue matters more than one high-ranked Binding. Five people with Guardian/helkir/three flexible roles are a common composition, not a forced rule or threat-table mechanism. [Content ontology](../live-model/NORMAL_DUNGEONS.md#rank-basin-and-environment); [Group/ecology owner](../live-model/COMBAT_ECOLOGY.md).

Raids allow at most ten human participants, with smaller parties legal. Six Normal standalone bosses are separate opportunities: mature R/O/Y/G/B/V, first-cycle R/R/O/O/Y/G. Expedition/Hard is a continuous six-wing attrition progression, not downscaled for initiates; eliminated participants stay out of that attempt with no mid-run replacements. Survivors may continue short-handed or abandon. Major awards are once per participant/boss/season, with mode identity and seasonal reset; assistance repeats are legal but do not repay the major award. Twelve opportunities means six Normal plus six Expedition, not universal guaranteed income. Exact Raid runtimes stay OPEN; roughly ten days for a first-cycle expedition is WORKING. Outside domai nucleate from Genesis storms into temporary non-isometric landscapes with a non-conscious White Crystal core. Core break causes progressive unraveling and evacuation/harvest pressure; the core is not a sentient controller. Valnak training incursions end as completed encounters at core break. R→V domai and outside persistent Dungeons remain distinct; no White domai follows from White Dungeon content. [Participation and spatial ontology](../live-model/DOMAI_PARTICIPATION.md); [Outside versus training ecology](../live-model/COMBAT_ECOLOGY.md).

Metric radii R3/O5/Y7/G11/B13/V17 miles, and author traversal equivalents at 1.7×, do not redefine local measurements. Nearby ≈5–7 ft stays coherent before depth/parallax flattening; luminous channel “tiger striping” and roughly 70% ordinary/30% expressed biology are visual author targets, with plants more expressed. Exact local scaling law remains OPEN. [Spatial metric](../combat-rewards/DOMAI_SPATIAL_METRIC.csv); [Spatial qualifications](../live-model/DOMAI_PARTICIPATION.md).

First validated kill starts a participant's seven-day eligibility window; conquest in-window can pay after departure, but no conquest means no conquest award. Exit/death incurs one full day of same-domai lockout. Contextual group contribution splits equally among eligible members, then individual ×0.8^deaths applies. Healing, control, support, operations and core assault matter alongside kills. Scoring, fractional rounding and optional bonuses stay OPEN BY DESIGN.
