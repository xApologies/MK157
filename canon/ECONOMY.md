# Economy and progression accounting


## What a credit and a price mean

Credits are persistent Valnak ledger entries, not XP or an imported currency. Earnings, grants, expenditure, earmarked reserves and actual cash balances are separate. Availability, affordability and chosen purchase timing are also separate: a character can buy cards or rest instead of immediately taking a rank. Organic world events → combat → gross income → discretionary spending → progression check is the authoring sequence; do not move combat or fabricate awards to balance a ledger. [Persistent ledger](../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#8-node-credential--persistent-dossier); [Authoring order](../live-model/27_CHECKPOINT_20_SOCIAL_LIFE_ARC4.md#6-consumer-economy--lock-direction).

All 1,016 Bindings, 229 summoned-entity prices and the nine Prime-price mirrors use existing semantic classes. Red anchors are Foundational 1,700 / Common Specialization 3,100 / Advanced 5,700 / Powerful 11,000 / Exceptional 17,000. Later ranks multiply the previously rounded rank by 1.60, 1.80, 2.10, 2.50, 3, 4 using decimal HALF_UP to whole credits at every step. No name/ID/basin classifier rerun is authorized. A locked price does not lock an ALPHA mechanism. [Full matrix](../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#fixed-class-matrix); [Class scope](../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#semantics-and-protected-fields).

The entry grant is 2,000. Pre-Legacy PC R→Blue costs 45,303 at full price; after acceptance the approved package pays 55% of each ordinary list rank, independently HALF_UP. Do not recursively discount discounted ranks or infer a universal discount for every Legacy. PC and Directed Coherence are Foundational; Coherence Field Common Specialization; Beam Powerful; Absorption and all nine Primes Exceptional. Prime cumulative ordinary R→W list cost is 4,308,616; illi's Red Absorption/each Prime costs 9,350. [Access and discount](../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#entry-and-white-legacy).

Kira's approximately 1,000 Armor anomaly remains separate. CSR/Orbs/Halo/Domain cost 61,017 each, with competence/access gates and no ordinary paid ranks. Later selected Black supports use that tier; names/count remain OPEN. The next node's 999,999,999,999 is temporary administrative pricing, not its natural price. [Black prices](../economy/KIRA_BLACK_ACQUISITION_PRICES.json); [Finale support/next-node scope](../live-model/32_CHECKPOINT_25_ARC7_FINALE.md).

Explicit author doctrine permits shallow incidental paid Trials in available personal time with between-wave termination. Formal ledgers model progression, not literal every-credit balances. No off-page total, shifted milestone, new required combat or erased recovery block is implied. Ordinary living is cheap relative to cards/Bindings.
