Its photospheric Genesis Log is a continuous macroscopic readout of actual neon states and throughput equilibrium, not a discrete universe power tier. R1/7 through V6/7 are reference landmarks; actual states can depart from them. White superposes the actual R–V states; Black/White are presentations of one Möbius-like organization in the current MK157 author model. Exact aggregation and Layer-0 correspondence remain OPEN; do not attribute this whole model to a particular _bricked release without evidence. Serial markers identify places, not scenes; do not generate names for every street or merge the distinct Genesis Card flagship into 001.
