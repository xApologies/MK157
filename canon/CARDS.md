# raeon and Genesis Cards


## Two physical cultures

Both use transparent durable Genesis-interactive maege glass, roughly standard trading-card size 2.5×3.5 inches as a WORKING form factor. They are not opaque colored plastic slabs. raeon is a playable topology/manifold game; Genesis Cards collect/display bounded representations of real Transductive manifolds. A Genesis Card does not grant or cast the represented Binding. A Fireball card could display R/O/G as an illustrative composition, not a rule for every Fireball. raeon color is game classification/rarity. Transparent geometry has depth, parallax, folding/overlap and quiet motion while fingers/world/light remain visible; it is resolved bounded topology, not a screen playing animation. Mathematical appreciation and casual beauty/rarity/history collecting coexist.
