# Kira


## Identity, craft and transformed biology

Kira enters Valnak at 22, genuinely Afflicted, after growing up in an armorsmith household. Mail is her exceptional manual craft: wire, mandrels, coiling, clipping, closures, weaving, repair, fit and articulation; many small links become one protective surface. This supplies strength, dexterity and an armorsmith's instinct for Armor, not prior combat expertise. She expects Creation; Elara routes her to Combat. Her viable evolutionary threshold is a WORKING model embedded in pathology, not a secretly healthy entry state or an acquired skill. The original character record remains the detailed owner: [Kira source](../../live-model/01_KIRA.md).

After Black restructuring she has energized, multilayered WHITE skin, healthy biological WHITE hair, BLACK eyes with internal R→V striation, effectively perfect symmetry, refined angular human features and enlarged orbital anatomy. The approximately 5'8"→5'11" change, lithe dense athletic build, crystalline Genesis skeletal/neural integration and continuity of identity survive. Hair fullness around 2–3× is working morphology. Exact microscopic anatomy, organs, mass, nails and skin-depth mechanism remain OPEN. White skin/hair can still invite Afflicted prejudice; her healthy depth and transformed eyes contradict it. This does not import Enix identity, potion/Class-2 causality, tattoos or an old rank ladder. [Biology owner](../../live-model/11_CHECKPOINT_06_KIRA_BIOLOGY_FIX.md#current-post-transformation-biology) and [morphology firewall](../../live-model/11_CHECKPOINT_06_KIRA_BIOLOGY_FIX.md#source-firewall).

Black/rainbow belongs chiefly to her active architecture, not permanent pigmentation. Armor reconfigures her own body-linked realization, including biological hair; it is not ordinary worn equipment. Five armored fingers come first; the three-claw configuration is learned and preserves the underlying fingers. Armor re-resolution is not biological healing. Repeated disruption costs throughput and fatigue, and momentum can still throw her. Matte-black biological skin, golden hair/eyes and a canonical gold Halo are superseded. [Body and Armor distinction](../../live-model/11_CHECKPOINT_06_KIRA_BIOLOGY_FIX.md#biology-versus-black-architecture); [visual correction](../../live-model/SUPERSESSIONS.md#checkpoint-06-normalization).

Diplomatic Pouch 01 centers her opening on **no longer dying**. Her parents' Valnak experience gives cultural familiarity; the roughly 11–17 expected Afflicted lifespan is an approximate author model. Elara's unexplained **“Curious.”** after inspecting her transformed eyes stays unresolved. Armor's barely visible buried spectrum, hairless full manifestation, proprioceptive five-fingers→learned three-claws control and lifetime-scale learning horizon are elaborated in the [current owner](../../live-model/01_KIRA.md#armor-of-the-abyss). The [personal primer](../../live-model/01_KIRA.md#personal-binding-primer) makes operation intuitive without granting mastery.
