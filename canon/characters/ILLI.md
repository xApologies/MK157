# illi


## Agency and starting architecture

illi is a princess with an independent life, original cohort, friends and family/royal obligations. Choosing Kira is an exercise of agency, not proof that her planned cohort is antagonistic. Kira draws her toward combat; illi increasingly helps Kira enter society. Their bond is sister-level; Kira and Elara remain BFFs. Dungeons matter to illi because they enable other citizens and provide service, community and sustained social contact. She is not simply Kira's passive support slot. [Independent lives](../../live-model/PARTNERSHIP_AND_CARRY.md#independent-schedules--income-topology--lock); [Relationship geometry](../../live-model/09_CHECKPOINT_04_ADDENDUM.md#relationship-geometry); [Service and community](../../live-model/31_CHECKPOINT_24_ARC6_GRADUATION.md#12-social--builder-continuity).

Her first Binding is Persistent Coherence Red. PC reaches Blue at O1D3, qualifying her for White Legacy acceptance; Absorption Shield Red follows O2D2. Blue PC costs 45,303 cumulatively at full price; the 2,000 entry grant is separate income. White Legacy is a curated compressed developmental opportunity, not instant White-ranked power. Approved purchases and ranks after acceptance pay 55% of each ordinary list price, independently HALF_UP. Omitted prerequisite Bindings are sandboxed rather than granted. [Entry economics](../../bindings/PRICING_MODEL.md#entry-and-white-legacy); [Compression meaning](../../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#3-white-legacy).

## Six capabilities, three compressed Prime gates

The current first-cycle six are Persistent Coherence, Absorption Shield, Genesis Beam, Genesis Prime, Coherence Prime and Resonance Prime/Juggernaut. The ordinary Coherence forms remain globally valid; the old plan to add Directed Coherence/Coherence Field to her six is superseded. Absorption is prevention, not healing. Beam adds selective offense but competes with shield refresh, sustained healing and emergency reserve. [Current six and sequence](../../live-model/ILLI_PROGRESSION.md#illi-corrected-progression-skeleton--lock); [Rejected older sequence](../../live-model/SUPERSESSIONS.md#checkpoint-08-governing-corrections).

## White direction and partnership limits

After B6D3 the goal is PC White, Absorption White and all three Primes White; Beam may remain Green. Remaining first-White-Legacy progression bill is 9,513,297. Exact purchase dates stay OPEN. After completing this Legacy she can pursue Decoherence; she voluntarily defers after Elara's warning rather than being blocked by a new mechanical restriction. [Arc Seven endpoint](../../live-model/32_CHECKPOINT_25_ARC7_FINALE.md); [Remaining bill](../../economy/ILLI_REMAINING_WHITE_BILL.json).

Project Princess Carry is affectionate, reciprocal and increasingly intense: early Armor carries, illi's prevention supports survival, then mature Orb throughput enables illi's expensive architecture. The mature defensive stack is Orb exclusion → illi absorption → Armor → Kira. Kira remains relentless rather than controlling; illi retains choices. The older Trial-heavy White projection is explicitly superseded by the White W1 three-run payoff and mature Green closer program. Registered participation earns illi her share legitimately; it is not gifted progression capital. No Trio/boosting-service scene is booked. [Current carry program](../../live-model/PARTNERSHIP_AND_CARRY.md#checkpoint-25-funding-and-ethical-closer-program).

Fixed White gross is 63,677,830; subtracting the remaining bill leaves 54,164,533 before discretionary purchases and any carried reserve/contextual income. Cards, dress ideas, friendships and possible Tiara Fund are meaningful choices; roughly 23,000 dresses are WORKING, not booked general prices, and savings percentage is OPEN. Neither gross earnings nor this remainder is a final bank balance. [White arithmetic](../../live-model/32_CHECKPOINT_25_ARC7_FINALE.md#fixed-white-gross); [Discretionary agency](../../world-clock/ARC5_HANDOFF.md#spending--social-liberty).
