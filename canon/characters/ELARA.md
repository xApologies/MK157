# Elara


## Personhood, runtime and knowledge

Elara is Valnak's personified social manifestation and Custodian, not a separate goddess, daughter or ruler. Valnak is a conscious domain, not a city housing an AI. Most people use the environment and Node; direct Elara interaction is rare, intimate and prestigious. Being widely known *of* does not mean everyone recognizes her face. [Identity](../../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#valnak--elara); [Character/social context](../../live-model/01_KIRA.md).

Between approximately 23-year cycles she studies civilization and participant provenance, then configures Legacies, incentives, training, services and interfaces. During the cycle automated runtime handles scale; she does not manually administer billions. Her privileged intervention coexists with results she dislikes but that obey her own rules. Thus Valnak can surprise her without becoming a separate mind. [Runtime owner](../../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#developerruntime-model).

Kira's compatibility reveals possibilities the system has never needed. Armor's anomalous starter pricing, BLACKOUT, legitimate Trial-credit outliers and Orbs used as cannonballs supply the comedy of an expert surprised by lawful consequences. Elara cuts the overload; finite local throughput and Kira's unknown saturation are distinct. She repairs later pricing without rescinding compatibility. Her knowledge remains large but incomplete, including Orb ultimate purpose and Kira's later development. [BLACKOUT](../../live-model/02_COSMOLOGY.md#valnak-draw--infinitely-deep-well-metaphor); [Comedy and lawful rewards](../../live-model/09_CHECKPOINT_04_ADDENDUM.md#elara-comedy-engine).

## Friend and cultural participant

Kira and Elara are BFFs; Kira and illi are sisters in relationship. Elara increasingly appears as a friend at Highlights, meals and social gatherings, not only an administrator. She cautions Kira against burnout in Arc Five. Her own Builder publications and concern with reactions are comedy/social texture; Custodian status does not guarantee a correct or popular build. No new scene, named peer or dated social event is created by these directions. [Relationships](../../live-model/09_CHECKPOINT_04_ADDENDUM.md#relationship-geometry); [Burnout and friendship](../../world-clock/ARC5_HANDOFF.md#social-thesis); [Builder participation](../../builder/COMMUNITY.md#elara-comedyculture).

The personal anti-boost request is an agreement grounded in friendship and respect, not a newly imposed system law. In the corrected finale no Trio is booked and the conditional post-Trio boosting conversation is not placed. After the second Green core close in White W1, Elara admonishes Kira for ending fresh campaigns before ordinary participants can contribute. Kira adopts mature-campaign-only closing. These are separate ethical boundaries; do not combine them into an invented enforcement system. [Anti-boost scope](../../live-model/03_VALNEK_PATHS.md#5-first-cycle-sandbox--green-graduation); [Fresh-core intervention](../../live-model/32_CHECKPOINT_25_ARC7_FINALE.md#white-w1--trial-payoff-and-core-cracking-discovery).

## Departure and protected understory

Elara can host/project presence through Kira's persistently sustained Orb and accompany her outside Valnak while remaining connected to Custodian duties. Exact identity, projection/extension/copy and instancing semantics stay OPEN. She becomes a Shaping/aera mentor and BFF, not a newly formal master. Kira later moves beyond established knowledge; no complete remote-Transducer understanding is granted at departure. [External presence](../../live-model/09_CHECKPOINT_04_ADDENDUM.md#elara-can-leave-valnak-through-an-orb).

At the finale the next Black node is temporarily priced at 999,999,999,999 until a later cycle under Elara's administration. This is not its natural price or a new ordinary rank. Preserve the author-understory/access boundary in the full Arc Seven source rather than giving characters automatic knowledge or inventing the node. [Next-node and author-understory owner](../../live-model/32_CHECKPOINT_25_ARC7_FINALE.md). Final Halo coloration, indefinite multi-Orb sustainment and Elara-through-Orb ontology remain unanswered; none follows merely from friendship or administrative power.
