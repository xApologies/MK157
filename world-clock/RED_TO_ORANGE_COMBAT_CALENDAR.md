## Required combat anchors

- **R3D2:** Raid group #1 clears Red (+2,500); repeated Orange attempts fail.
- **R6D6:** group #2 repeats that Red boss (+0 major reward), then clears Orange (+3,750). This creates no shared cap across distinct same-rank bosses.
- **O1D6:** new-season Red Raid clear (+2,500) under existing seasonal reset.
- **R5D1–2:** domai excursion; **R5D3** full-day recovery; zero conquest payout.
- Orange Dungeon repetition is intentional first-cycle group ecology. Optional failed Yellow attempts can use OPEN days later with no required completion income or mandatory extra economic events.

## First-cycle ecology and authoring

Green Solo grants early first-cycle sandbox graduation and veteran grouping access. Red Season groups overwhelmingly work in Red/Orange; Yellow Dungeon groups are difficult to form and generally underprepared. Kira/illi are outliers with repeatable Yellow Duo Trial capability. Kira's early limit is population/spatial saturation despite exceptional melee throughput; she lacks mass-clear/area-control architecture.

**WORKING ceilings:** Kira Solo Yellow roughly W8–10; Kira/illi Duo Yellow roughly W8–10; Trio inefficient and potentially stalled mid-Orange. illi makes rare weak Solo attempts because PC is support-heavy; the scaffold has two experiments. These ranges do not replace global Trial timing/population or final standings.

Kira/illi gain a reputation for unusually persistent combat/death exposure and aggressive grinding. Valnak remains a marathon. Optimize narrative variety and plausible progression, not credits/hour. Dungeons build groups and future Raid/domai contacts; failure/recovery can consume time without income. Resurrection removes permanent death in Valnak, not pain, fear or psychological cost. Runs are clock facts, not chapter mandates. Project Princess Carry and independent lives remain.
