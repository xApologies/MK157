## Why illi chooses Coherence Prime

Arc Five makes a group-healing limitation visible.

illi's sustain works very well when Kira absorbs most pressure, but Dungeons/Raids create distributed damage:
- sloppy positioning;
- failed role execution;
- multiple simultaneous injuries;
- Kira pouncing away from the formation;
- longer Green-level attrition.

illi must juggle:
- Persistent Coherence;
- Absorption Shield;
- Genesis Beam;
- emergency reserve.

Her fatigue ceiling is not automatically "the healer's fault"; poor group execution can manufacture excessive healing demand.

Coherence Prime is attractive because it approximates a second autonomous helkir/healing actor and distributes sustain workload.
The need is meaningful but not urgent enough to force immediate purchase.
