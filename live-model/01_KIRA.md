# Kira --- Affliction, evolution, transformation, design

## Before Valnak

Kira is 22 and reaches Valnak genuinely **Afflicted**. Earlier same-day
material saying she was only mistaken for Afflicted is superseded.

Working evolutionary model: Genesis-collapse/Affliction conditions have
exerted biological pressure over generations. Many mutations/variants
never stabilize. Kira is the first known Afflicted whose relevant
biological/genetic change crosses a viable threshold. She is still
pathological on entry; evolution has not yet separated the useful
phenotype from Affliction. Her changed biology permits unprecedented
Black compatibility. She is a transitional first successful phenotype,
not necessarily the final evolutionary form.

This does not grant knowledge, skill, or perfect control.

## Blacksmith identity

Kira was raised in an armorsmith household and wants the
Creation/Craftsman route. Her specialty is mail. From childhood she
learns wire preparation, mandrel/coiling, clipping rings, closures,
weaving, repairs, garments, fit and articulation. At 22 she is an
exceptional mail armorsmith: fast, precise, low-waste, highly dexterous,
excellent at seamless repairs and fitting. She loves many links becoming
one protective surface.

Smithy work gives functional strength, endurance, strong
forearms/back/legs, dexterity, and familiarity moving ingots, tools,
armor and stock.

## What Valnak sees

Valnak first recognizes Afflicted, then detects something unprecedented.
Elara perceives it through being Valnak. The normal
compatibility-pruning system does not find the expected ceiling. Elara
tries to persuade Kira to try Combat rather than immediately choosing
Craftsman.

Kira is presented with six unprecedented Black offerings; **Armor of the
Abyss** attracts her because she is an armorsmith.

## BLACKOUT transformation

Valnak attempts the Black Binding/chirality restructuring. Normally a
bounded restructuring reaches saturation. Kira does not.

The operation opens her into Black/Abyss. She begins accepting/drawing
energy beyond Valnak's expected load. Power-supply analogy: Valnak may
have a 1000-watt available throughput while Kira behaves like a
2000-watt demand.

She does not empty the Abyss. She drives **Valnak's available
draw/throughput** to its ceiling. Valnak visibly dims/browns out, like
lights during a grid disturbance. Elara performs an emergency override
and cuts the process.

Before cutoff, Kira is comprehensively remade: R--V-organized human
realization → Black organization.

The Afflicted pathology is gone afterward. Kira remains human, but her
stable body is now a Genesis-energy realization pushing the bounds of
human materiality.

## Genesis-Orb blender — promote to active canon
Kira's Genesis Orbs are persistent external Black Genesis-energy bodies, roughly cannonball-sized (~8 in / 20 cm), dense/solid in interaction and capable of acting as mobile Transducers.

Kira's signature Trial use is the **Orb blender**:
- one or more Orbs resolve into extremely rapid orbital trajectories around Kira / defended local space;
- this creates a moving physical/Genesis-interactive exclusion/disruption geometry;
- it is **NOT a force field**;
- ELDRIS entering the orbital path physically collide with dense, high-speed Black Genesis Orbs and suffer catastrophic blunt/Genesis trauma;
- open Trial arenas provide unusually favorable unconstrained geometry;
- multi-Orb patterned control progressively makes ordinary melee-wave pressure pathological for Valnak's assumptions.

Kira therefore breaks the Trial while obeying its rules.

Ordinary wave pressure:
`more ELDRIS → more simultaneous threats → eventual saturation`

Mature Kira in an open Trial:
`more ELDRIS → more bodies attempting to cross the Orb exclusion geometry`

As long as control/sustain/geometry remain favorable, wave quantity no longer scales against her normally. Later progression can become limited more by time, cognitive load, fatigue, boredom, or willingness to continue than immediate defeat.

Exact final Eternal Standing wave numbers remain OPEN. Do not canonize Wave 96 merely because it was used conversationally as an example.
