## 9. Tea House / Tea Party noble institution — LOCK
This is noble social/civic culture and persists inside Valnak as well as outside it.

Around age 17, a noble mother customarily establishes/purchases a residence for her daughter. This residence functions as the daughter's **Tea House** and materially establishes an independent household.

The daughter chooses/establishes an associated charity. Over time it may mature into a foundation with donations, invested/endowment capital, staff, research and independent recurring income.

The Tea House establishes **independence**.
The Tea Party establishes **social identity/network**.
The charity/foundation establishes **civic/economic agency**.

The cultural purpose is not separation from eventual marriage. It establishes the woman as an independently capable actor before marriage so that marriage can operate as a union/partnership of two established networks. Husband and wife may reinforce one another's formal, commercial, political, social and institutional deals while neither social network is erased.

Tea-party relationships can persist across decades/long lives and become durable social/institutional infrastructure.

Noble women practice this at the highest level. Extremely wealthy non-noble/merchant families may imitate the custom, generally with less capital/institutional reach.

Kira becomes a desirable Tea Party invitation both because of her own celebrity/anomaly and because her attendance makes illi/the princess more likely to attend.
