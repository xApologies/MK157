This is a recurring rhythm, not a daily obligation. The nightly World Clock event continues at its existing time even when they stay home.

They maintain a constant presence at **two distinct flagship spaces**: the raeon flagship for playable cards, deck building and tournaments; and the Genesis Card flagship for collectible manifolds. The latter is not silently merged into map location 001, and no new map serial/address is assigned without an authored location. Champion Table/Highlights dinner remains a frequent narrative return point, not an every-night attendance requirement.

Home, lounging, recovery and genuine “nothing” days are positive calendar states. Combat, death/pain exposure, celebrity attention and obligations require decompression. An OPEN day does not imply a hidden event, shopping trip or automatic Solo run. Specific recovery obligations continue to govern where already scheduled.

## Tea House, Tea Party, charity

Around **age 17**, a noble mother customarily establishes or purchases a residence for her daughter. This **Tea House** materially establishes an independent household. The daughter chooses or establishes an associated charity, which may mature into a foundation with donations, invested/endowment capital, staff, research and independent recurring income.

| Institution | Cultural purpose |
|---|---|
| Tea House | Independence and an established household |
| Tea Party | Social identity and a durable network |
| Charity/foundation | Civic and economic agency |

The purpose is to establish the woman as an independently capable actor before marriage. Marriage can then be a union and partnership between established networks. Husband and wife may reinforce each other's formal, commercial, political, social and institutional deals; neither network is erased. Tea-party relationships can persist over decades and long lives as institutional infrastructure.

Noble women practice this at the highest level. Extremely wealthy merchant or other non-noble families may imitate it, generally with less capital and institutional reach. This is a custom and conceptual institution, not an invented mandatory statute or universal age threshold.

Kira is a desirable Tea Party invite both for her celebrity/anomaly and because her attendance makes illi/the princess more likely to attend. Invitations do not dictate attendance or make every day a royal obligation. No particular Tea House, charity name, assets or personal ownership event is assigned to either character here.

## Research, protected implementations and recurring income

Mathematics is materially operational: it describes Genesis-field structures and underlies `ru’ne`/sigil architecture. A mature foundation may manage donations and invested/endowment capital, fund a mathematics/research department, develop or own protected mathematical/sigil implementations, license them, receive royalties, and reinvest returns in its charitable mission and institutional growth. Research/patent/licensing income is an approved direction; a particular patent grant is not invented.

Common/public mathematics and sigil infrastructure coexist with proprietary/specialized implementations. These can provide independent productive capital associated with the woman/foundation, rather than automatically collapsing into a husband's wealth at marriage.

Exact patent statutes, legal mechanisms and terms, royalty percentages, ownership formalities, investment regulation, assets and income totals remain OPEN. No outside currency/royalty amount is converted into Valnak progression credits by this doctrine.

## Valnak social-credit transfer firewall

Valnak permits gifts/transfers for civic, social and non-progression consumption: residences, clothing, meals/hospitality, ordinary amenities/services, raeon cards, Genesis Cards and comparable non-progression resources. A wealthy family may fund a daughter's residence or social/civic life.

Transferred or gifted wealth may **not** finance Binding acquisition, Binding ranks, combat/rank progression or equivalent progression shortcuts. Indirect routing does not create permission for a parent or benefactor to finance progression. The independent-progression and institutional eligibility rules remain intact.

Exact enforcement, credit provenance/tracking, transfer UI, limits and anti-circumvention implementation remain OPEN. Permission to give social gifts does not assign any particular gift, sponsor or funding source to Kira's 86,000-credit card, and cannot be used to fabricate an Orb-financing loophole.
