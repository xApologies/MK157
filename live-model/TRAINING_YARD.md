# TRAINING_YARD — live-model promotion

## Trial exit rule

A Trial wave is committed combat.
- A short break/timer exists between waves.
- During the inter-wave break, a participant/group may voluntarily terminate the Trial.
- Once the next wave begins, there is no voluntary mid-combat exit. The wave resolves through victory/death under existing Trial/re-instancing rules.

## Training Yard

The **Training Yard** is the practice mode layered over Trial wave architecture.

- Solo, Duo and Trio formations may use it.
- A participant/group may select a starting wave that the relevant participant/formation has already completed/unlocked.
- Training Yard starts do **not** award Trial credits.
- It exists for technique practice, new-Binding/build testing, party drills and controlled exposure to known pressure bands.
- Exact effect on official standing/unlocking beyond the selectable-start rule remains OPEN; do not invent an advancement exploit.

Normal credit runs remain different:
- start at W1;
- completed waves pay the ordinary per-person schedule;
- participants may stop only during inter-wave breaks.

## Kira behavior

Kira often refuses the convenient Training Yard start because she wants the credits anyway.
Early Arc Five she repeatedly starts ordinary Solo Trials at W1 while learning Genesis Orbs:
- uses one Orb crudely;
- gets overloaded;
- reverts to Armor/CSR/claws to finish the current wave;
- terminates during the break;
- resets to W1 and tries again.

This is deliberate skill practice, not a sudden regression in capability.
