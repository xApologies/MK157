# Valnak domai participation — Checkpoints 18 and 22

Valnak domai contribution awards remain **OPEN BY DESIGN** and **are not halved** by Trial/Dungeon/Raid recalibration. Large contextual awards are appropriate for civilization-scale operational behavior. There is no fixed rank table or deterministic validated-contribution scoring formula.

## Eligibility and payout

The participant's **first validated eldris kill** starts a **7-day contribution eligibility window**. If core break/conquest occurs within it, contextual contribution pays even if the participant has already left. Leaving does not erase contribution. If conquest does not occur within the window, no conquest contribution payout follows from that participation window.

Voluntary exit **or** death imposes **one full day of suspension/recovery from that domai**. Death additionally multiplies eventual payout by **0.8 per death**:

Checkpoint 22 clarifies the participant award as the equal eligible base share of a contextual group award:

`base_member_share = group_award / eligible_members`

`individual share after deaths = base_member_share × 0.8^deaths`

An optional individual bonus layer, its interaction with this share and rounding remain OPEN; none is booked.

This lockout is scoped to the same domai. Voluntary departure alone adds no death. Validated scoring and fractional-credit rounding remain OPEN. No eligibility renewal/stacking or additional clock/UI mechanics are inferred.

Validated contribution includes eldris kills, healing/support, control, operational contribution, core assault and other validated participation. The local ledger and core-break bonus concept remain; exact bonus amount/formula remains OPEN. The first-kill eligibility trigger does not replace broader contribution with kill-only scoring.

These are **Valnak domai** rules. Expedition Raid attempt-long elimination/no mid-run replacement remains separate. Valnak domai ranks remain R→V with cumulative legal eldris and no White eldris. Main White Crystal break ends the training encounter. Outside domai retain their existing core and progressive-unraveling ontology.

## Checkpoint 22 — group award and discretionary liquidity

Valnak evaluates validated group/formation contribution, then divides the contextual group award equally among eligible members. A Kira+illi Duo receives 50% each before individual death adjustments. A large military formation may earn much more overall but divide it across many members; small-group size itself does not increase pay. [Group economy](../combat-rewards/DOMAI_GROUP_ECONOMY.md) and [structured rules](../combat-rewards/DOMAI_GROUP_ECONOMY.json) clarify the existing [participant rule record](../combat-rewards/DOMAI_PARTICIPATION_RULES.json); all 7-day eligibility, one-day same-domai lockout and ×0.8^deaths rules remain. Exact contribution scoring, rounding and optional bonus layer stay OPEN.
