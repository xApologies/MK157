# MK157 — Genesis Cards and physical card cultures

Current authority: Checkpoint 14 commodity doctrine, retaining Checkpoint 11 card semantics. No new Binding records or universal basin mappings are assigned.

## 7. Two distinct card ecosystems

MK157 has TWO distinct card cultures:

1. **`raeon`** — playable topology/manifold card game.
2. **Genesis Cards** — collectible trading/display cards containing bounded representations of Transductive manifolds.

Do not conflate them.

## 8. Shared physical card form

Both systems use durable transparent **maege-glass** cards.

Working physical form:
approximately standard trading-card/baseball-card size, roughly **2.5 × 3.5 inches**.
Exact millimeter dimensions remain nonessential unless later needed.

Maege glass is:
- transparent;
- extremely durable;
- Genesis/energy interactive;
- suitable for anchored field/manifold structures.

Do NOT depict these cards as opaque colored plastic slabs.

## 9. Genesis Cards — direct MK-147 inheritance plus MK157 elaboration

Direct inherited core:
**Genesis Cards are collectible trading cards holding/displaying bounded manifolds; distinct from `raeon`.**

A Genesis Card anchors a bounded representation/projection of a real Binding/Transductive manifold into the maege-glass card.

The card does NOT grant or cast the Binding.
It is a collectible/display/observational representation.

The manifold itself is the principal visual content/artwork.

### Genesis Card color semantics
Genesis Card color is STRUCTURAL, not rarity.

The colors visible inside the transparent card correspond to the actual R/O/Y/G/B/V energy basins required by the represented manifold topology.

Example:
If a Fireball manifold requires Red + Orange + Green basin structure, its Genesis Card can display a dynamic translucent Red/Orange/Green interplay.

Do not encode a fake universal element-color mapping.

### Visual behavior
Genesis Cards remain transparent enough to see the world/fingers/light through the glass.

Inside that transparency:
- colored energy threads/fields/geometry occupy apparent depth;
- multiple basin colors can overlap without becoming opaque blocks;
- the bounded manifold can appear multidimensional;
- parallax can reveal different relationships as the card tilts;
- geometry may seem to pass through/fold around other geometry;
- energy/relationships can move or oscillate quietly;
- the card is NOT a screen playing an animation;
- the structure is a resolved bounded Genesis topology.

The result should be visually beautiful, dynamic, translucent and mathematically/fantastically strange.

A knowledgeable Transductionist/Architect may appreciate the topology itself; a casual collector may simply value the beauty/rarity/history.

Genesis Cards exist outside Valnak.

Valnak can also release specialized **Valnak-cycle-exclusive Genesis Card sets** during Seasonal Valnak.

These are a separate collector ecosystem from the Valnak Cycle `raeon` set.

Exact:
- set size;

- release cadence;
- print/production quantities;
- pricing;
- shop location;

remain OPEN until needed by story/calendar work.

They provide another legitimate discretionary credit sink.

## 11. `raeon` card visual/color semantics

`raeon` remains the playable game and defers detailed mechanics to `_raeon`.

Its physical cards use transparent/durable maege glass.

For `raeon`, the Red -> Orange -> Yellow -> Green -> Blue -> Violet (and established White where applicable) color presentation is a GAME classification/rarity identity, not a statement that the card's represented topology requires those energy basins.

FIREWALL:
**Genesis Card color = actual basin composition of the represented manifold.**
**`raeon` card color = game classification/rarity.**

Do not merge these semantics.
