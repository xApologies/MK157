# 1. Governing principle

This is NOT conventional fantasy "crafting."

**CRAFT KNOWLEDGE + MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE + TRANSDUCTION = ADVANCED CREATION**

A Creator is not playing a crafting minigame.

A Creator develops civilization.

# 2. Creation as primary Valnak route

Valnak does not exist solely to train combatants.

Broadly:
- **Combat protects civilization.**
- **Creation develops civilization.**

Creation therefore legitimately has:
- Trials / competency evaluation;
- progression;
- credits;
- reputation;
- shops;
- commissions;
- discoveries;
- economic activity;
- Binding development.

Creation is not a consolation path for people who cannot fight.

# 3. Creator identities

Established examples include:
- blacksmith / armorsmith;
- material fabrication;
- Alchemist;
- ru’ne / sigil specialist / rune carver.

Other crafts can exist where knowledge and physical/Genesis transformation justify them.

Do NOT manufacture a giant RPG profession list just for completeness.

# 4. Architects — related but distinct

Architects overlap philosophically with Creation because they construct Genesis-field structures, but Architect remains its own unusual discipline.

Architects are field mathematicians / engineers / topologists working with:
- Genesis-field topology;
- manifolds;
- knots;
- boundaries;
- constraints;
- `velis` / anchors;
- persistent field structures.

Do not flatten Architect into "another crafter."

# 5. Rune carvers / civic sigils

Rune carvers/sigil specialists build physical civic sigil infrastructure.

Large civic sigils can be enormous — football-field-scale pieces of infrastructure or comparable major installations rather than tiny decorative glyphs.

Multiple civic sigils can be distributed around a city and integrated into one stabilizing architecture/domain.

Working world function:
these structures stabilize the local Genesis Field enough that ordinary Genesis-collapse behavior is suppressed within/around the protected city domain.

Rune carvers and Architects can collaborate but remain distinct:
- rune carver: physical sigil/ru’ne realization;
- Architect: field topology, anchors/`velis`, manifold relations.

Agricultural field engineering can also dramatically accelerate harvest/growth through Genesis-field infrastructure. "Seven harvests in a normal one-harvest period" is an author-side working example; exact agronomy is not locked here.

# 6. Creator Trial

The initial Valnak Trial establishes demonstrated competency.

Combat Trial asks: can you fight?

Creator Trial asks: can you actually make things?

The Creator selects/is routed to an appropriate practical discipline such as armorsmithing, blacksmithing, Alchemy or ru’ne work.

The Trial evaluates actual competency rather than imported wealth.

# 7. Creator Trial developmental logic

Core progression:

**REPRODUCE -> REPAIR -> CONSTRUCT -> ADAPT -> SOLVE -> CREATE**

This logic is more important than rigid minigame tables.

## REPRODUCE
Can the Creator correctly reproduce known work?

For a smith:
- prepare material;
- reproduce component;
- reproduce known structure;
- reproduce known weave.

## REPAIR
Can they understand existing work well enough to repair it?

Repair tests understanding, not memorization.

## CONSTRUCT
Can they make a complete functional object?

## ADAPT
Can they solve an unfamiliar constraint?
Examples:
- unusual geometry;
- strange material;
- customer requirement;
- damaged stock;
- unfamiliar pattern.

## SOLVE
Can they solve an open-ended fabrication problem?

## CREATE
Can they produce something genuinely new?

At the highest level Creation becomes discovery.

# 8. Same principle across disciplines

Alchemy can progress through:
reproduce reaction -> correct failure -> produce known material -> adapt process -> solve unknown material problem -> discover.

Ru’ne work can similarly progress:
reproduce -> diagnose -> repair -> construct -> adapt -> solve -> create.

Exact task-to-color tables remain OPEN.

# 9. Creator rank

Creator competency may use the cultural:
Red -> Orange -> Yellow -> Green -> Blue -> Violet -> White

This is demonstrated Creator competency, not simply "Red object / Orange object."

Exact universal metrics remain OPEN.

# 10. No busywork reward

Valnak does not grant meaningful progression merely because a Creator spent eight hours repeating low-value work.

Valnak evaluates:
- competency;
- successful work;
- difficulty;
- adaptation;
- contribution;
- innovation;
- discovery.

# 11. Basic Creator economy

Core loop:

MATERIAL/STOCK
-> CREATOR
-> FINISHED WORK
-> VALNAK EVALUATION
-> CREDITS / PROGRESSION
-> ORDINARY MATERIAL RECYCLED

This allows civilization-scale practice without infinite piles of duplicate equipment.

# 12. Ordinary output

Ordinary practice work can be evaluated and recycled back into stock/material.

A smith can therefore produce thousands of practice pieces over decades without Valnak filling with garbage.

# 13. Exceptional output

Exceptional work can escape recycling because it is:
- unusually useful;
- innovative;
- technically exceptional;
- historically important;
- beautiful;
- culturally valuable.

Possible outcomes:
- preserved example;
- customer piece;
- public work;
- exceptional artifact;
- Auction lot.

# 14. Commissions

Creator shops support real commissions.

A customer brings a requirement. The Creator solves the problem.

Commission work can contribute to:
- credits;
- progression;
- reputation;
- technical experience;
- customer relationships.

The customer receives the finished work.
The Creator receives recognized value for the contribution.

# 15. Creators do not mass-produce for credit farming

A Creator does NOT primarily enter Valnak to manufacture endless armor and get rich by repeatedly selling inventory.

The progression model is competency/development:
- practical checkpoints;
- evaluated work;
- commissions;
- novel constraints;
- discovery;
- high-value contributions.

Valnak may sell/reproduce accepted designs, but Creator progression is not "craft 10,000 swords."

# 16. Creator credits / Binding development

Creator work is a legitimate credit/progression engine.

Credits are not reserved for killing `eldris`.

Creators can use earned credits for:
- Creator development;
- relevant Bindings/shapings;
- shop activity;
- broader Valnak purchases.

Do not invent a separate crafting currency.

Creators may later diversify into offensive/utility Bindings once their craft architecture is mature. Combatants may similarly develop secondary creation capability. In social terms, "Creator" and "combatant" remain major developmental orientations rather than absolute class prisons.

# 17. Why Creators matter outside Valnak

Valnak can sell excellent equipment while Seasonal Valnak is open, but outside the cycle human Creators are essential for:
- repair;
- maintenance;
- modification;
- replacement;
- new commissions;
- new material processes;
- civilization-scale infrastructure.

This is another reason Creation is a primary civilization route.

# 25. Smithy vocabulary

Use **smithy** for the blacksmith's workplace.

A forge is traditionally one tool/component of a smithy.

A high-level Valnak smith does not automatically need:
coal, bellows, smoke, soot, giant fire or constant hammering.

# 26. Central blacksmith concept

A high-level Valnak blacksmith is:

**A CONDUCTOR OF MATTER**

This is the central conceptual identity.

# 27. Conductor of matter — meaning

A blacksmith understands:
- material composition;
- metallurgy;
- structure;
- geometry;
- temperature;
- phase/state;
- stress;
- deformation;
- joining;
- cooling;
- Genesis interaction.

Transduction gives more direct control over those relationships.

The craft remains blacksmithing because knowledge is still mandatory.

Powerful Transduction + bad metallurgy can still produce bad work.

# 28. Heating metal

A Transductive smith does not require combustion to heat metal.

Energy can be conducted into material directly.

The smith can control:
- where heat goes;
- input amount;
- temperature distribution;
- local state;
- cooling behavior.

# 29. Shaping metal

Skilled smiths can manipulate:
- curvature;
- thickness;
- alignment;
- local deformation;
- joining;
- stress distribution;
- material movement;
- fine geometry.

Through:
material knowledge + physical technique + Transduction.

This is not generic "metal telekinesis."

# 30. Tools remain

Transduction does not erase physical tooling.

Possible tools:
- work surfaces;
- mandrels;
- gauges;
- jigs;
- fixtures;
- measuring tools;
- hand tools;

At master level tools may become support rather than the defining mechanism.

# 31. Hammering

Hammering is an optional technique when useful.

manual hammering != blacksmithing

At very high competency a master may directly manipulate raw material through shapings and never physically touch a conventional hammer during a particular piece.

# 42. Valnak production vs human Creation

Valnak can convert energy to matter and produce extremely high-quality standardized equipment.

Valnak-made armor can be excellent across the familiar Red->White cultural/rank grammar.

Human Creation still matters because Valnak's purpose is not to replace human creativity.

A master Creator can produce a piece that is:
- individualized;
- technically novel;
- culturally meaningful;
- beautiful;
- recognizable by maker;
- a true original.

Valnak can reproduce a validated human design for the Shop.

Important continuum:
- Valnak-standard design;
- Valnak reproduction of validated human design;
- Creator-produced original;
- bespoke master commission.

A reproduction can be technically excellent while the one-of-one original retains provenance/art value.

# 17. Seasonal Auctions

Valnak hosts a major Auction at the end of **each seven-week season**:
Red Auction, Orange Auction, Yellow Auction, Green Auction, Blue Auction, Violet Auction, White/Final Auction.

The Auction is a spectacle and social event even for people who cannot afford the lots.

Auction inventory can include:
- exceptional Creator originals;
- historically/culturally notable works;
- one-off Valnak-produced objects;
- rare equipment;
- unusual Alchemical preparations;
- rare `vaen`;
- Genesis materials;
- prepared/infused metals;
- scarce components;
- other limited/provenance-rich objects.

Economic distinction:
**Shop = reproducible availability**
**Auction = scarcity / provenance / novelty / exceptional contribution**

Valnak can reproduce a validated human design for the Shop while the original one-of-one Creator work remains culturally/economically distinct.

Bidding uses Valnak credits.
