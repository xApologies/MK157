## 12. Prime Elementals
Prime Elementals are a genuine pinnacle Summoner taxonomy, not protagonist-only summons. Existing 180 ordinary elemental manifestations remain intact.

Definition: humanoid/person-like specialist manifestation; non-sentient but highly autonomous; long-duration (many hours at sustainable load); broad integrated capability within its specialty; approximates an extra specialized party member; specialty-specific power; high output raises fatigue; persistence is load-dependent; even Red is persistently useful; ranks improve efficiency/coherence/autonomy/throughput/high-load endurance.

Initial taxonomy:
- PE-001 Genesis Prime Elemental — offensive Genesis Expression specialist.
- PE-002 Coherence Prime Elemental — helkir-equivalent restorative specialist.
- PE-003 Resonance Prime Elemental — Juggernaut/frontline persistence specialist.
- PE-004 Dimensional Prime Elemental — spatial/corridor/vector/redirection specialist.
- PE-005 Chirality Prime Elemental — chiral organization/admissibility specialist; capability strictly downstream of actual Chirality Bindings.
- PE-006 Combustion Prime Elemental — combustion/thermal specialist.
- PE-007 Lightning Prime Elemental — electrical/plasma specialist.
- PE-008 Mineral Prime Elemental — mineral/metal/material specialist.
- PE-009 Atmospheric Prime Elemental — gas/pressure/flow specialist.
Genesis Field Prime was considered and NOT adopted.

Ordinary Prime access is a deep capstone: multiple prerequisites may need WHITE merely to unlock Prime purchase at RED. This can require multiple 23-year cycles. Ordinary practitioners retain those White prerequisites as a secondary toolkit.
