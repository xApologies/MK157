# aithren, vaelum and Accord Registry — Checkpoint 18 inheritance

Checkpoint 18 imports applicable current MK-147 institutional doctrine. Reviewed at upstream commit **ae7c8b406370b752fd5e6f809be0148315294379**; [review/source snapshots](../provenance/CHECKPOINT_18_MK147_REVIEW.json) pin the evidence. MK157 explicit canon controls conflicts.

`aithren` is a lowercase individual/cultural adventurer-participation identity, not merely a job title. Participation, discovery, competence and contribution carry cultural status. Dungeon delvers and Genesis-storm chasers are established contexts.

`vaelum` is the lowercase persistent organization under which aithren may operate: independent, formally recognized and contract-capable. It is distinct from a Crown military unit, noble house or geographic place. Members can remain independent operators while working through one.

The **Accord Registry** is a Crown administrative domain registering and formally recognizing vaelum. **Crown → Accord Registry → recognized vaelum → aithren members/teams** describes recognition/organization, not universal military command. Broader Registry authority stays OPEN.

## Operations, contracts and reputation

Clients can include the Crown, nobles, cities, merchants/caravans, Institutions of Knowledge, researchers, physicians, private citizens and other lawful institutions. Work includes security, escort, Dungeon expedition, storm/domai support, eldris suppression, exploration, retrieval/recovery/harvesting, dangerous transportation/logistics, civil/medical/agricultural/infrastructure support and unusual one-off operations.

For a research expedition, the researcher controls the research objective; the hired vaelum supplies routes, security, logistics, safe work areas, material recovery and emergency extraction. Contracts and organizational reputation are accepted; demonstrated competence can affect access to demanding work.

The Crown may encourage decentralized capability through qualifying economic/property/housing/facility benefits or tax reductions without putting all aithren on permanent government payroll. Exact rates, qualifying categories and tax code remain OPEN. Upstream approximately-200-member headquarters examples are illustrative, not a required size.

Exact internal governance, contract/quest boards, arbitration and reputation scoring stay OPEN. Node-authenticated facts support selection without replacing judgment.

## Transfer boundaries

Normalize active generic guild wording only where it means this institution. Distinct military/noble organizations and historical source wording remain.

Excluded: Genesis Point guild-ELN; King's Highway/old Accord travel; permanent Domai Heart/Chimera ontology; Sanctuary architecture/suppression rings; bandit suppression cubes; old prime-density/Neon currency systems. Existing MK157 Binding-circle, Kira/Enix identity and other source firewalls remain. Outside domai ontology is unchanged.
