## Starscape visual

A starscape exists within a vast black **Abyss/ocean of energy**.
Externally it resembles a dark spherical universe / black-hole-like
bounded object floating in the abyss. Its luminous photospheric boundary
may glow anywhere continuously from Red through Violet, including
intermediate hues.

## Genesis Log

The photosphere is the **Genesis Log**, a macroscopic visual readout of
underlying neon state and energy-transition equilibrium.

Equilibrium landmarks: R=1/7≈0.142857; O=2/7≈0.285714; Y=3/7≈0.428571;
G=4/7≈0.571429; B=5/7≈0.714286; V=6/7≈0.857143.

These are reference anchors, not mandatory realized values. Real neon
states normally sit off exact equilibrium. A Red-associated contribution
might be \~0.28 rather than exactly 1/7. A greenish-blue Log means an
aggregate tendency through the Green↔Blue region, not a discrete "Green
universe." Exact aggregation math remains OPEN.

The Genesis Log also expresses how much energy the starscape/singularity
transitions while suspended in the Abyss. Author analogy: the starscape
**breathes**---energy flows in and out while maintaining dynamic
equilibrium. The Log constrains/expresses available singularity
throughput.
