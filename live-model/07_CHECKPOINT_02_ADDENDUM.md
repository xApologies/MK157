## Valnak population topology
Valnak is a civilization and sanctuary, not merely an event.

Core thesis: **Help civilization survive, and civilization's sanctuary eventually becomes yours.**

**Permanent Valnak** is a separate residential/civic domain for people who earned sanctuary. **Seasonal Valnak** consists of temporary/returning participant cities active during openings. Seasonal premium property persists for its owner across openings until retirement into Permanent Valnak or death, then becomes available.

Permanent and Seasonal domains are residentially/administratively separate but socially porous during seasons. Permanent residents may visit, teach, work, shop, mentor, socialize, spectate and compete in Seasonal Valnak.

Fresh **First-Cycle Initiates** cannot group with veterans/Permanent residents. After First Cycle, Seasonal veterans and Permanent residents may freely form mixed Prism/dungeon/training/crafting teams. Residency status is not a separate open-competition class.

Retirement means dangerous service is no longer owed, not that contribution must stop. Permanent residents may voluntarily teach at Blackstone, mentor, craft, research, coach Prism, operate businesses, etc., and Valnak can reward this with credits. Exact sanctuary qualification and post-death persistence/reconstruction remain OPEN.

## Blackstone
Blackstone is Valnak's institution of knowledge, warfare education, tactics, strategy, research and training. It bridges individual capability and organized warfare. It teaches ELDRIS behavior, battlefield awareness, formations, dungeon warfare, sustainment, command/communication, withdrawal, casualty management and integration with military/guards/adventurers.

Faculty can include century-retired veterans. Blackstone is architecturally a beautiful university campus integrated with gardens/water, not a fortress/citadel/Pentagon.

Military organizations rotate personnel through Valnak while retaining outside continuity forces because ELDRIS do not pause during openings. Entire formations can enter to retrain. Valnak performance is visible to recruiters.

## Children's Valnak
Under 17s can enter a protected developmental domain. Children experience tactical fear/consequences without adult visceral mutilation/pain: a struck arm can go numb/unusable; a struck leg can go dead; severe hits remove them from a Trial.

Development emphasizes Creation/Craftsman, Architect, Helkir/healing, utility/nonlethal Transduction, and bounded child-safe summoning/companions. Valnak constrains admissible use, not merely labels: child healing cannot simply invert into biological destruction; metal manipulation cannot casually weaponize a worn breastplate.

At 17 adult lethal/visceral Combat development becomes available; existing competence remains.

## Seven seasons
Valnak has seven seasons: **Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White**. Exact internal calendar/time dilation remains OPEN.

## Seasonal city
Seasonal Valnak is mixed-use, not functionally zoned. Glass/dark metal, vertical gardens, rooftop trees, canals/lakes/streams/waterfalls, plazas, parks and bridges dominate. Shops, restaurants, entertainment, craftspeople, residences and social spaces intermingle.

Density peaks organically toward the center and decreases toward garden-city/estate shared residential landscapes. Private isolated residence domains (island/forest/beach/etc.) exist separately.

Real-estate principle: **Space is cheap; shared presence is valuable.** A private island is easy to instantiate; a penthouse over a genuinely populated plaza or prestigious workshop address is scarce/expensive.

Node/map relocation handles direct travel. Physical travel remains desirable; elegant autonomous carriage-like transit exists as a working concept, exact implementation OPEN.

## Craftsman's Row / Blacksmiths' Row
Craftsman's Row is a famous embedded destination, not a district.

Blacksmiths' Row working geometry: two-tier plaza; 8 smithies per tier per side = **32 premium smithies total**. Clean open Transductive ateliers, no smoky medieval forge requirement. Masters are conductors of matter and their work is public artistry/performance.

Atelier value comes from audience, reputation and social address. Valnak can incentivize crafting orders; craftsmen sell armor, weapons, alchemical products, devices and art for credits.

## Prism
Prism is a major sport/social mixing mechanism. It is NOT a conventional stadium field. Working minimum environment scale ~**2 × 3 miles**. Programmable terrain includes cliffs, walls, pillars, water, forests, ravines, elevations, platforms and open maneuver space because competitors may fly, run extremely fast, jump far and use Transduction. Spectator/support facilities live mainly around the perimeter/elevated observation positions.

## Binding terminology

## Discounts vs LEGACIES
Canonical in-world term replacing “Path” is **LEGACY**.

A one-off **Offer/Discount** is a subsidized Binding/package with no long-term contract.

Working one-off ladder:
- Red: 1 Binding at 80% normal cost.
- Orange: 1 at 70%.
- Yellow: 2 at 80% each.
- Green: 2 at 70% each.
- Blue: 3 at 80% each.
- Violet: 3 at 70% each.

A **Legacy** is a contractual Valnak-designed developmental sequence. Accepting means agreeing to take prescribed Bindings in order for the subsidy. Valnak uses Legacies to prevent stagnation, exploit aptitude, fill civilization capability gaps and guide useful group/military/adventuring development.

Valnak is civilization-scale benevolent but unsentimental about using people as tools. Acceptance is voluntary; incentive architecture is deliberately manipulative.

Working finite commitments, not rigid yet:
- Red ~3 Bindings.
- Orange ~3 with stronger subsidy.
- Yellow ~4.
- Green ~4 with stronger subsidy.
- Blue ~5.
- Violet ~5 with stronger subsidy.
Legacies may chain repeated color-rated packages; exact sequence varies by person. A participant on one may culturally be called **a Legacy**.

### White Legacy / True Legacy
White is categorically different. A **White Legacy** is an open-ended/total developmental architecture: Valnak pre-plans essentially every Binding acquisition while commitment continues. It is a guided life rather than a finite package.

A person who accepts it becomes culturally a **True Legacy**. True Legacies are extraordinarily rare (working rarity: one or two every few centuries) and include legendary heroes/figures of old. They persist in military history, noble houses, Blackstone teaching, artifacts and folklore. True Legacy denotes extraordinary Valnak developmental investment, not moral goodness.

### Black Legacy
Kira repeatedly receives **Black Legacies**, not ordinary R–V/White Legacies. Black is not “rank eight,” and Kira is not automatically a True Legacy. Society has no historical category.

A Black Legacy means Valnak generated a coherent developmental architecture for which civilization has no historical human category.

Recurring comedy: Kira sees the black-neon plaque and gets excited; Elara effectively responds, **“Don't touch it.”** Elara immediately checks which Bindings, how many, how many seasons, restructuring, throughput, what Valnak thinks Kira is becoming, and energy cost. The first Black architecture already browned out Valnak.
