# Valnak Normal Dungeons — current through Checkpoint 17

## Rank, basin and environment

Valnak Normal Dungeon content ranks are Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White. White is a mastery/content rank and introduces **no White eldris**: its complete legal palette remains R→V. Checkpoint 16 superseded the earlier no-White-Valnak-Dungeon restriction for Normal training content. Checkpoint 17 also supplies White Hard rewards and the same rank-legal palette and average area. Outside-world persistent Dungeons and outside/Valnak domai ontology are unchanged; no White domai are introduced.

Basin color is literally an expression of an eldris's Genesis Field energy identity. Capability follows resolved architecture: Red physical; Orange intrusion; Yellow ranged force; Green juggernaut/persistence threshold; Blue expressed projection; Violet high Genesis expression. Author combat families remain R/O/G melee and Y/B/V Transductionist. No generic stat inflation, aggro/threat mechanics or illegal higher-basin eldris are introduced.

**Domain ≠ Rank ≠ Realization.** Domain is environmental archetype, rank governs the legal basin palette and content/mastery demands, and realization is the generated tactical instance. Rank does not recolor the environment.

These targets assume an appropriately ranked, reasonably competent party and are not stopwatch guarantees. With the established 31-hour day, 156 hours is five days plus one hour; the supplied ~5 days is rounded.

Over-ranked veterans can clear low-ranked content much faster; Valnak does not rubber-band content upward. One high-ranked Binding does not make the whole build that rank: breadth, depth, competency, fatigue capacity and integration matter. Multi-day operations need not include ordinary-human nightly sleep blocks; exact extreme sleep/food/recovery physiology remains OPEN.

## Population and author map guidance

Dungeon eldris are **pre-populated** across the generated realization, rather than continuously delivered as Trial waves. They can roam, hunt, react, converge, separate and occupy terrain. The existing Trial arena and wave-delivery rules are unchanged.

The [atomic library](../builder/encounters/eldris/README.md) is reusable vocabulary: individual eldris → atomic group (1–4) → encounter composition → Dungeon/Trial/domai population plus spatial placement. Larger compositions combine/duplicate groups within their legal ceiling. The master update assigns no final placements or total populations.

Author-story maps should favor readable entry → progression → endpoint topology. Limited/reconnecting branches are fine; excessive dead-end backtracking is undesirable because it changes runtime. This is author guidance, not a universal geometric law.

## Nine environmental domains

1. **Deep Caverns** — natural, predominantly traversable subterranean chambers, passages and shafts; no unexplained cities, ruins or constructed architecture unless specifically requested. Waterways are optional natural detail, not automatically dominant.
2. **Dune Wastes** — dunes, cliffs, canyons, mesas and open desert.
3. **Oldgrowth** — dense mature forest, ravines, streams and huge trees.
4. **The Overgrown City** — established pale monumental/civic stone streets, buildings, rooftops, plazas and infrastructure heavily reclaimed by nature, not generic modern post-apocalypse.
5. **Highlands** — mountains, ridges, valleys, cliffs, plateaus and passes.
6. **Marshlands** — wetlands, channels, islands, reeds, mud and submerged ground.
7. **Frozen Expanse** — snow, glaciers, frozen lakes, crevasses and ice caves.
8. **Broken Coast** — beaches, cliffs, islands, sea caves and tidal channels.
9. **Ruined Citadel** — defensive/fortified complex of walls, towers, courtyards, gates, halls, bridges and subterranean fortifications; may share cultural architectural ancestry.

Exploratory Deep Caverns image-generation attempts, including failed Yellow+ renders, are NONCANON and are not imported. Existing approved base geography remains separate from these attempts.

## Completion economy

White Normal Dungeon successful-completion base is **68,910 credits = 53,910 + 15,000**. The first term is the preserved W1–35 Trial total; the second is the completion premium. No extra party-split, individual distribution, bonus or first-clear rule is inferred from this base.

Reliable Dungeon clears should generally outperform equivalent Trial farming in credits/hour. Failure, procedural and coordination risk preserve the value of reliable repeatable Trials. The complete [Normal/Hard completion and Raid reward tables](../combat-rewards/README.md) are locked by Checkpoint 17. Dungeon distribution, partial-progress, boss/contribution and first-clear bonus formulas remain OPEN. Valnak domai awards remain contextual and OPEN BY DESIGN.

Hard uses the same average areas **5/13/25/53/113/285/450 mi²**, the same rank-legal R→V palettes (including at White), and the same 209 atomic groups. Hard increases population density and the number of deployed groups, creating sustained combat, fatigue and coordination pressure. Its exact **1.5× completion payout** does not multiply area or establish a runtime multiplier. Exact Hard runtime targets, instance geometry, population counts and placement remain OPEN.
