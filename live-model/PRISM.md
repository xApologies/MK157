# 18. Prism domain

The northeast PRISM region on the Valnak map is the dedicated Prism sporting territory.

Working map footprint: roughly ~7 miles across within the ~20x20-mile city map.

This is not necessarily one fixed field. Valnak can partition the area into multiple bounded Prism play domains or allocate a larger combined area to a major match.

Ordinary Prism playfields can be enormous by Earth athletic standards — working author examples around 1.5x2, 2x2, or 3x3 miles — because Transductionist athletes may possess flight, CSR, elemental propulsion/air manipulation, extreme Augmenter speed, etc.

The sport is three-dimensional. Elite players can make huge spaces feel tactically small.

Spectator architecture includes elevated observation areas / skybox-like viewing structures.

# 19. Prism credit economy

Participants can legitimately enter Valnak primarily to compete in Prism.

Prism awards credits based on validated match participation/contribution and success.

Possible contribution categories include:
- team victory;
- objectives;
- defensive plays;
- opponent disables/tags;
- assists;
- mobility/control contribution;
- other role-specific validated performance.

Exact per-action values/multipliers remain OPEN.

Do not require combat Trials/Dungeons for a Prism specialist to have a meaningful Valnak progression route.
