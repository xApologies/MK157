# KIRA BLACK SYSTEMS — mastery doctrine and Arc Five Orb stage

## Governing Black rule

Kira's Black acquisitions are **systems**, not ordinary Red→White Binding ladders.

**No ordinary rank-up ladder. No defined mastery ceiling.**
Acquisition opens the system; progression is discovery, competency, integration and mastery.
Mastery is the long-horizon goal, not a finite final rank.

Armor of the Abyss is the clearest proof: Kira is still using only a narrow early vocabulary — armor + claws — and has not remotely exhausted the system.

The systems remain:
1. Armor of the Abyss
2. CSR / Continuous Spatial Resolution
3. Genesis Orbs
4. Halo
5. Domain

Late Valnak begins revealing that these systems can compose into an **integrated battle system**.
Do not prematurely unlock that mature integration in Arc Five. It continues developing after Valnak and through the rest of the book.

## Genesis Orb range/control doctrine

Genesis Orbs have no defined intrinsic range cutoff.
The practical limit is Kira's control.

As distance, velocity, Orb count and trajectory complexity increase, useful control becomes harder.
If Kira exceeds her current control envelope, manifestation can persist while precise motion degrades:
an Orb may slow, drift, hover uselessly or settle rather than disappearing.

The more she practices, the farther and faster she can operate the Orbs while maintaining useful control.

## Arc Five — one-Orb mini-blender

Arc Five develops **one-Orb competency**, not mature Orb mastery.

Sequence:
crude cannonball → controlled flight → crude orbit → Kira-centered anchored orbit → mini-blender.

Mini-blender working author model:
- one Orb;
- roughly **11-foot radius** around Kira;
- semi-consistent/stable angular velocity;
- localized to Kira and translates with her under CSR;
- moderate speed at this stage;
- primarily displacement/knockback + meaningful damage, not automatic annihilation;
- trajectory can destabilize if Kira is hit, distracted or overloaded;
- descriptive "kinetic shield" language is acceptable, but it is NOT a force-field Binding.

This is the competency threshold that changes Kira's Solo Trial standing.

## Blue Solo tactic — "jumping spider"

Kira ports CSR and the mini-blender into a hit-and-run priority-target tactic:
1. identify Blue ranged/Transductionist groups;
2. CSR pounce;
3. mini-blender disrupts the local cluster;
4. claws/close combat remove priority Blue targets;
5. repeat against remaining Blue and dangerous Yellow ranged groups;
6. once ranged architecture is sufficiently degraded, finish the melee ecology where Kira is strongest;
7. re-engage new ranged groups as the wave continues.

Failure remains plausible:
- ranged hit during movement;
- crash/trajectory disruption;
- Orb falls out of synchronization;
- melee closes while Kira restabilizes;
- cognitive overload.

## Duo Blue wall

Duo spawns greater encounter population while paying the same per-person wave credits.
Kira cannot simultaneously assassinate all ranged threats and keep illi safe from the larger population.
illi becomes the vulnerable saturation point; Kira gets pulled defensive; ranged pressure survives; the formation collapses.
The Duo therefore stays at W18/W19 through this stage.

## Port into Dungeons

The Blue-Solo tactic is not a separate Dungeon invention. Kira ports it directly into five-person content.

Role transition:
pseudo-Guardian/frontliner → **mobile assault / damage dealer / forward disruption**.

Dungeon loop:
- surge ahead with CSR;
- dismantle cohesion of roughly 3–4 groups when topology permits;
- remove/disrupt priority Blue/Yellow ranged elements;
- leave damaged/dispersed groups for the four-person trailing formation;
- reverse/backtrack;
- collapse into the remnants from the front while the party pushes from behind, creating a pincer.

This does NOT mean Kira solos the Dungeon.
Dungeon topology matters: open regions favor her; tight corridors/ceilings can trap or constrain the orbit/escape geometry.

Arc Five implication:
- Yellow becomes increasingly comfortable for strong groups.
- **Green Dungeon attempts become credible** with a capable progression-cohort composition.
- Green failures: G4D2/G4D6. First clear: G5D3. Second/third clears: G5D4/G5D7.

## Mature future blender — preserve

The later mature blender is a different scale:
- multiple Orbs;
- much larger radius (eventually hundreds of yards/meters under mature control);
- much higher velocity;
- enemies in open Trials may never approach Kira/illi closely.

That mature geometry is intentionally Trial-breaking.
Do not back-port mature performance into Arc Five.
