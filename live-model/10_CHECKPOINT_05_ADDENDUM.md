# Checkpoint 05 Addendum — Valnak Seasons, Binding Economy, Transduction Infrastructure, Fatigue, Summons, and Builder

**Authority:** Checkpoint 05 is newer than Checkpoints 01–04 on explicit conflicts; otherwise canon accumulates.

## Seven-season Valnak cycle
Valnak uses seven spectrum seasons: **Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White**. Current working duration is **seven weeks per season**. Red–Violet use pleasant pale/pastel sky coloration. White uses a bright-world starscape: the environment remains illuminated while the sky resolves as an exceptionally clear dark field dense with visible stars.

Dungeons, Trials, Raids, and domai are events that consume meaningful time. Dungeons may last multiple days. High-wave Trial runs can consume many hours or days; this supports Kira eventually terminating extreme runs because of time/boredom rather than defeat. Exact per-wave timing remains a normalization detail.

## Binding economy
Every Binding has its own intrinsic **Red base cost B**.

Recursive rank price:
- Red = B
- Orange = previous × **1.60**
- Yellow = previous × **1.80**
- Green = previous × **2.10**
- Blue = previous × **2.50**
- Violet = previous × **3.00**
- White = previous × **4.00**

Example only, B=1,000:
R 1,000 → O 1,600 → Y 2,880 → G 6,048 → B 15,120 → V 45,360 → W 181,440.

**Breadth is accessible; depth/mastery is expensive.** Ordinary first-cycle participants do not casually leave with collections of White Bindings. Deep specialization can require multiple 23-year Valnak cycles.

The older illustrative “300-credit fireball + 700-credit personal shield” example is superseded as an economic model.

## Rank behavior
Red→Violet develops the same Binding through mechanism-appropriate efficiency, throughput, output, precision, range, capacity, duration, concurrency, control, and sustainable fatigue cost.

**White adds a Binding-specific secondary expression derived from the same mechanism.** It is not merely a larger Violet.

Established examples include Persistent Coherence → Bloom (consume remaining anchored reserve for burst restoration) and Genesis Beam → sustained channel behavior.

## Six Transduction domains
Primary Binding taxonomy:
1. **Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence**
2. **Dimensional Geometry**
3. **Genesis Field Topology**
4. **Genesis Expression**
5. **Resonance**
6. **Chirality**

`maege`, Summoner, Augmenter, helkir, and Architect are professions/build tendencies, not the primary ontology.

### Architect / Genesis Field Topology
Architects are mathematicians/field engineers. They directly learn field topology, manifolds, knots, boundaries, constraints, anchors/`velis`, stability, degradation, and construction. Their work is usually prepared rather than improvised during chaotic close combat. Military campaigns and domai incursions are natural Architect environments. Do not pad Architect with a conventional combat-spell list.

### Genesis Expression
The large external/offensive domain: Genesis beams/orbs, projectiles, beams, rings, lances/shards as geometry, blasts/bursts, fields, barriers, and physical/elemental expression.

Elemental Transduction is physical/Genesis manipulation, not a metaphysical fire/earth/air/water ontology. Fire, lightning, plasma, water, gases, metals, minerals, pressure, acoustics, etc. are valid when lawfully realized.

### Resonance
Major home of Augmenter development: reinforcement, synchronization, sustained biological enhancement, and related coupling. Limited overlap with Persistent Topology is allowed because both interact with biological Genesis structure.

### Chirality
Organizational grammar/admissibility of resolved structures. Supports locks, constraints, restructuring, counter-Transduction, and at extreme mastery structural dissolution/unresolution. High-end Chirality may dismantle a target's chiral organization until the prior persistent structure no longer resolves and its energy/state redistributes.

## Absolute basin rule
Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Violet are **energy basins / structural energy identities**.

They are NOT Transduction domains, elements, fixed power levels, or class identities.

Never encode mappings such as “Red = Resonance.” A Binding uses whichever basin combination its field topology requires. Basin combinations may be authorially assigned as structural identities without deriving the full underlying Chirality-Fabric mathematics.

## Fatigue and basin lockout
Combat is fundamentally **a war of attrition over sustainable Transductive capacity**.

Offense, healing, augmentation, summoning, barriers, mobility, etc. compete against the user's finite sustainable capacity.

Individual basins can also fatigue/exhaust. If a required basin becomes unavailable, every Binding requiring that basin becomes unavailable until recovery. Thus emergency healing can cost a combatant offensive capacity; Augmenters may have to drop reinforcement; Summoners may collapse manifestations; Builder must consider basin contention and lockout blast radius.

## Coherence / Decoherence lock
Exactly six current foundational Persistent-Topology Bindings:

Coherence:
1. Directed Coherence — heal this target now.
2. Persistent Coherence — anchored heal-over-time on the target's Genesis Field.
3. Coherence Field — bounded restorative region.

Decoherence:
4. Directed Decoherence.
5. Persistent Decoherence.
6. Decoherence Field.

Do NOT reintroduce bone/tissue/organ healing subdivisions or generated filler such as Vital Hold, Recovery Reserve, Triage Priority, Stabilization Mark, Healing Efficiency Link, or Field Triage Sense. Injury type changes complexity/throughput/fatigue, not the fundamental healing operation.

## helkir / Combat Medic / Absorption
`helkir` is the healer profession. A civilian physician can be a helkir.

`combat medic` is an author-side ALPHA role label for a combatant combining the coherence kernel with prevention/support and potentially offense.

Absorption is prevention rather than healing and is tactically valuable because it prevents trauma/incapacitation before biological injury resolves.

Helkir↔Augmenter overlap is intentionally limited: healing can incidentally reinforce the repaired region; augmentation can incidentally stabilize/minorly restore reinforced tissue. Neither replaces the other's specialization.

## Summoning
Summoned entities are resolved Transductive manifestations rather than souls pulled from another plane. Common architecture integrates:

**Genesis Expression + Resonance + Chirality.**

Elementals are non-sentient but autonomous within validated behavior/command packages.

Low-output manifestations may persist for hours. Large/high-output manifestations may be practical only for minutes or seconds. Exhausting any required basin collapses the manifestation and locks out unrelated Bindings sharing that basin until recovery.

A summon Binding evolves R→White as one manifestation architecture; do not create seven separate entities merely for rank.

The root `summons/` registry supplies candidate manifested-entity profiles.

## Builder
Valnak's node includes **Builder**, a build-planning environment. Builder lets participants inspect Bindings, select target ranks, estimate credit investment, assemble packages, inspect compatibility/conflicts, evaluate sustain/burst load, evaluate basin contention/lockout consequences, inspect Summoned Entity profiles, and browse curated Legacies.

Valnak can surface Trending, popular/high-rated, compatible-with-current-build, Valnak Recommended, and civilization-priority trajectories. Recommendations are incentives, not removal of free will.

## Legacies
A Legacy is a curated/validated developmental trajectory, not a rigid class. The root `builder/paths/` registry contains candidate Combat Medic, Augmenter, `maege`, and Summoner Legacies built from actual Binding and Summoned Entity IDs.

Legacy rank Red→White describes maturity/depth of the package. Individual Bindings retain independent ranks and White expressions. Architect remains outside ordinary Legacy generation.

## Ordinary progression / finite content
Valnak is not an infinite credit-farm sprint. Dungeons may consume days and are psychologically/physically demanding. Participants take downtime, train, socialize, explore, and recover.

Raid bosses/rewards are finite content for their availability window rather than endlessly farmable credit faucets. This pacing makes low-rank breadth common and deep Violet/White specialization prestigious.
