# Checkpoint 04 Addendum — Black Legacy Pipeline, Eternal Standings, and Post-Valnak Continuity

**Authority:** newest canon; cumulative with Checkpoints 01–03. Explicit newer statements supersede conflicts.

## Five foundational Black Bindings
Kira's prescribed foundational Black Legacy contains:
1. Armor of the Abyss
2. CSR — Continuous Spatial Resolution
3. Halo
4. Genesis Orbs
5. Domain

Each prepares Kira for the next: Armor trains cognitive embodiment; CSR spatial/dimensional control; Halo energetic throughput/load-bearing; Orbs externalized/parallel control; Domain direct interactive Genesis Field access. General progression: **access → control → fluency → integration**.

## Black Legacy economics
Accepting Armor of the Abyss commits Kira to the prescribed foundational Black Legacy. It is not free.

At initial evaluation Valnak offers the five ordinary directions (Augmenter, Summoner, Helkir, Architect, Maege/Transductionist) plus unprecedented Black Armor. Because Black is outside ordinary pricing knowledge, Valnak initially surfaces Armor at an anomalously low starter-scale price, working figure ~1,000 credits. Elara intervenes and repairs the economic environment for later Black offerings without invalidating Kira's compatibility.

Later Black Bindings require meaningful credits (working scale thousands/tens of thousands; exact table OPEN) and sufficient competency/admissibility. Thus progression has both a credit gate and competency gate.

## Domain graduation
Domain completes the compulsory foundational Black sequence. After Domain, Kira can increasingly choose individual Black support/interface operations. She can also learn/invent shapings herself through Domain if she gains the necessary knowledge and control. Valnak remains faster/safer because it can provide validated implementations.

Kira gravitates toward tank/support/battlefield-interface development because she enjoys grouping, protecting allies, peeling, and making parties function.

## Domain outside Valnak
Kira continues progressing outside Valnak. Domain lets her study the Genesis Field directly. Physical touch can let her accept a contacted structure into immediate resolved interaction without first projecting a large-radius Domain. With learned Helkir/coherence knowledge she can, for example, touch a wound, resolve it, and heal through Domain. She is not automatically a Helkir; knowledge and control remain required.

## Genesis Orb persistent sustainment
With ordinary Halo support, **one Genesis Orb can be maintained continuously/full-time with negligible energetic fatigue**. Active control still consumes cognition. Whether two or three can be sustained indefinitely is OPEN.

Genesis Orbs can eventually serve as remote Transductive/Domain anchors. Kira initially fails to appreciate this and continues wielding them as cannonballs/orbital bludgeons.

## Elara can leave Valnak through an Orb
Elara discovers she can host/project her consciousness through Kira's persistently sustained Genesis Orb, allowing her to accompany Kira outside Valnak.

Exact identity/instancing semantics remain OPEN; do not silently decide whether this is projection, extension, copy, etc. Practical canon: Elara can be present with Kira outside while remaining connected to her Custodian role.

Outside Valnak Elara becomes Kira's mentor in Shaping and **AERA**, while remaining her best friend rather than becoming a formal master figure.

## Relationship geometry
- **Kira + Elara = best friends / BFFs.**
- **Kira + illi = sister-level bond.**
- Elara remains part of the Kira/Elara/illi social party outside Valnak through the Orb.
- illi's royal obligations mean she does not routinely adventure outside Valnak, but her sister relationship with Kira remains socially/politically important.

Kira still visually reads to outsiders as Afflicted because of white skin/white hair, despite her transformed Genesis biology. illi treating Kira publicly as family creates a social contradiction for nobles: Afflicted prejudice versus the princess's chosen sister. Elara's relationship, Kira's fame, and Black Legacy intensify the ambiguity.

## Eternal Standings
Canonical name: **Eternal Standings**.

Valnak maintains current seasonal standings plus permanent all-time historical Eternal Standings, at minimum for Solo, Duo, and Trio Trials.

Kira's Genesis-Orb "blender" can create absurd historical outliers in open wave arenas. Exact final wave numbers are OPEN; do not canonize Wave 96 until wave/rank mathematics are fixed. Records can note voluntary withdrawal/termination rather than defeat.

Expected narrative outcome:
- Kira: top Solo Eternal Standing.
- Kira + illi: top Duo Eternal Standing.
- Kira + illi + an as-yet-unidentified recurring third friend: top Trio Eternal Standing.

## Trial-breaking Orb interaction
Open wave arenas are structurally vulnerable to Kira's mastered high-speed Orb orbit. ELDRIS entering melee range collide with dense Black Genesis Orbs. This is not a force field; it is a physical/Genesis-interactive moving exclusion/disruption geometry.

Kira eventually reaches the point where Solo/Duo/Trio progression is limited more by time/boredom than defeat.

Duo defensive stack:
Orb exclusion → illi White absorption → Armor of the Abyss → Kira.

Trio can carry a third friend to enormous progression/credits.

## Why Dungeons/Raids/domai remain useful
Dungeons constrain Orb geometry with walls, corridors, corners, ceilings, objectives and party members. Kira must learn precision, changing orbit, remote trajectories and actual Transducer use.

Progression by theater:
- Trials: overwhelming local control.
- Dungeons: precision / constrained geometry.
- Raids: coordinated group execution.
- domai: battlefield scale.

## Elara comedy engine
Elara is constantly frustrated because:
1. Kira breaks Valnak mechanics while obeying the rules.
2. Valnak itself correctly generates unprecedented Black results because Kira satisfies conditions the system never expected.

Examples include anomalous starter pricing, later Trial-credit explosions, Genesis Orbs used as cannonballs, Halo removing energetic constraints, and Eternal Standings preserving the results.

Valnak rewards Kira legitimately, often financing the next Black Legacy step that lets her break another assumption.

## Post-Valnak time and handoff
Working scale: participants experience roughly about a year across seven Valnak seasons while only a couple months pass outside. Exact conversion remains OPEN.

Kira exits Valnak with:
- five foundational Black Bindings;
- incomplete but extensive mastery;
- Domain and selected interface/support operations;
- extraordinary Eternal Standing fame;
- illi as sister-level royal relationship;
- Elara as BFF, traveling Orb presence, and Shaping/AERA teacher.

Valnak is the launch point. Outside Valnak, Elara teaches established Genesis knowledge; Kira practices and eventually reaches Genesis behavior beyond Elara's existing knowledge.

**Valnak gives Kira the tools → Elara teaches the known field → Kira eventually reaches the unknown field.**
