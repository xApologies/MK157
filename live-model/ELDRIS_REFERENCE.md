# eldris — active ontology and combat reference

Checkpoint 21 promotes the compatible behavior/ontology in the [supplied import](../provenance/checkpoint-21-package/ELDRIS_CANON_IMPORT.md) and [MK147 source PDF](../provenance/checkpoint-21-package/source/MK147_MASTER_ELDRIS_LRS.pdf), read in full (12 pages). Source sections 0–10/13–18 support collapse ontology, appearance and behavior. Preserve lowercase **eldris** in active MK157 prose.

An eldris is a stabilized, persistent recursive-collapse structure: local Genesis/chirality instability runs recursively, becomes bounded by closure, and persists as an entity. The process stabilizes rather than stopping. Physical substance is energy-mass closure, not ordinary flesh. Bodies characteristically appear black, wispy/rootlike/smoky/branching/fractured; basin identity leaks through eyes, wounds, inner traces, impact effects and behavior, not body pigment. Basin-dominant closure and collapse body are distinct aspects.

| Basin family | Source behavior pattern | Tactical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Physical contact: claws, teeth, body-force | Contact is dangerous even at the lowest family |
| Orange | Contact plus energy intrusion | Fatigue, destabilization, internal damage and healing-load pressure |
| Yellow | Energy-mass projection: orbs, beams, rings, projected force | Ranged knockback/force; spacing, cover, shielding/barriers and line-of-sight discipline |
| Green | Resonance/structural reinforcement; larger, heavier juggernaut persistence | First major power jump; slow kills consume attention while other populations close |
| Blue | Advanced Yellow-family expressed energy projection | Exploding, burning, shearing, detonating and secondary effects after impact |
| Violet | High Genesis expression; mage-like manifestation | Legendary, poorly understood threat |

The source's broad threat categories are **A: R/O/Y; B: G/B; C: V**. Its qualitative jump intuition is `R -- O -- Y ==== G ==== B ====== V`. These are eldris behavior/threat descriptions, **not universal color→domain assignments**, character ranks, a damage formula, species classes or fixed capability statistics. Basin identity remains ontological and actual capability depends on architecture. The existing melee-family R/O/G and Transductionist-family Y/B/V labels remain author composition metadata.

The PDF's S3 language is structural intuition, not final mathematics. Its spirit analogy does not introduce a new spirit system; illustrative dungeon population counts do not set MK157 spawns, encounters or rewards. No unrelated MK147 plot, identity, Binding-circle or progression mechanics transfer. All **209 atomic groups**, 1–4-body records, cumulative R→V palettes and IDs remain byte-identical. There are **no White eldris**. Existing Normal/Hard R→W permissions and outside Dungeon/domai rules remain governed by MK157. ## Arc Four application

Pre-Orb Kira with Armor of the Abyss + CSR:
- Green juggernauts are killable with Abyss claws but consume enough time/attention to let other populations close.
- CSR prevents simple lock-down but aerial escape exposes Kira to Yellow/Blue ranged lines.
- cascading failure can occur when a projected-force hit knocks Kira down into converging melee.

illi after Genesis Beam Red:
- remains sustain/support first: Persistent Coherence + Absorption Shield;
- adds selective ranged offense, especially against Yellow projection threats;
- must now manage fatigue between Beam expenditure, Absorption refresh and emergency reserve;
- Beam improves W17/W18 consistency rather than automatically solving Blue.

Pre-Orb Trial doctrine for Arc Four:
- Kira Solo: W18 Green clear; can enter W19 Blue and fail.
- Kira+illi Duo: W18 Green clear; can enter W19 Blue and fail.
- Blue is the next major wall; reaching it is groundbreaking first-cycle performance but not Blue-capable operation.
