# MK157 Summoned Entity Compendium v0.1

**229 candidate manifested-entity profiles** for Valnak Builder/story use.

- 180 elemental manifestations: 18 lawful physical/Genesis mechanisms × 10 entity geometries.
- 40 non-elemental creature-pattern manifestations.
- All are non-sentient but autonomous within validated command/behavior packages.
- Core Transduction integration: **Genesis Expression + Resonance + Chirality**.
- Energy basins are assigned as structural/topological identities only. Basin color does not mean an element, Transduction domain, or power level.
- Low-output manifestations may persist for hours; extreme manifestations can exhaust their required basins in seconds/minutes.
- If any required basin exhausts, the manifestation collapses and other Bindings requiring that basin are unavailable until recovery.
- Rank evolves the same manifestation from Red→White; ranks are not seven separate summons.

This is an ALPHA candidate registry intended to populate builds and give the story a deep summon vocabulary. It is not a requirement that all 220 appear in prose.
