# Canon Locks — v0.2

## Six-domain taxonomy
1. Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence
2. Dimensional Geometry
3. Genesis Field Topology
4. Genesis Expression
5. Resonance
6. Chirality

Bindings are catalogued by Transduction domain. `maege`, Summoner, Augmenter, helkir, and Architect are build/profession affinities, not the primary taxonomy.

## Absolute energy-basin rule
Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Violet are energy basins/energy structures. They are NOT Transduction-domain labels. A Binding requires whichever basin combination its actual field topology requires. v0.1's generated basin assignments were cleared rather than silently preserved.

## Coherence / Decoherence lock
Exactly six current foundational Bindings:
- Directed Coherence
- Persistent Coherence
- Coherence Field
- Directed Decoherence
- Persistent Decoherence
- Decoherence Field

Do not reintroduce Vital Hold, Recovery Reserve, Triage Priority, Stabilization Mark, Healing Efficiency Link, Field Triage Sense, or Coherence Diagnostic as healing Bindings.

## Architect
Genesis Field Topology is primarily Architect territory. Architects directly construct/manipulate field topology, manifolds, knots, anchors/velis, and mathematical structures rather than relying on a padded combat-spell catalog.

## Summoning
Summons/elementals commonly integrate Genesis Expression + Resonance + Chirality. Low-output manifestations may persist for hours; high-output manifestations may exhaust required basins in seconds/minutes.

## Chirality
Chirality is organizational grammar/admissibility. High-end fantasy use may dismantle a target's chiral organization until that persistent structure no longer resolves; this is not arbitrary domain/color magic.

## Vocabulary
Canonical spelling: `maege`, not mage.
