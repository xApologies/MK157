# MK157 Live Model --- Valnak Architecture

## Ontology and Elara

Valnak is a **domain of existence with intrinsic consciousness**. It is
not merely a place containing a separate intelligence. It can read
provenance, evaluate, grant credits, restructure people, instantiate
trials, preserve/restore projected participants, create/recycle matter,
maintain cities/residences, update Paths, and learn. It is
extraordinarily capable but not omnipotent.

**Elara** (E-L-A-R-A) is Valnak's personified social manifestation,
recognized as the **Custodian of Valnak**. She is not a separate
ruler/goddess/daughter. Valnak may instantiate Elara simultaneously in
multiple places. Her familiar presentation is female. Most people
interact with Valnak through the environment/node; direct Elara
interaction is rare and intimate, and friendship with her is culturally
prestigious.

## Cycle and purpose

Valnak opens once every **23 years** on a predictable date. Inside are
seven seasons: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, White. External
open duration and exact internal time ratio remain open.

Valnak is a safe utopian civic domain; ELDRIS do not roam its cities.
Dangerous trials/dungeons are deliberate training environments. Valnak
trains civilization to survive/fight ELDRIS outside.

Valnak also supports eventual permanent community: people may retire
there after sufficient service and may ultimately live with those they
protected. Recycling/re-resolution is possible for identities not
perpetuated; no Hell is required. Exact afterlife mechanics remain
mysterious.

## Time Shell / ledger

Every person possesses an intrinsic Time Shell/provenance record and a
personal resolved domain. It records thoughts/intentions, actions,
experiences, responses, events affecting them, nearby environment, and
their path through life. Domain scale varies; exact math is open.

Valnak can read it. Thus Valnak knows aptitude, desire, deeds, outside
ELDRIS service, and context. It does not need self-report.

Valnak does not police every human act; it controls its gifts. Someone
who deliberately uses Valnak-granted power to prey on humanity may
return and have those powers stripped/revoked, then be returned to human
jurisdiction. Serious offenders may avoid Valnak thereafter, creating a
self-exiled underworld.

## PSSP/stasis and death training

On entry, embodied state is preserved/stabilized and experienced
participation occurs through a high-fidelity PSSP/Valnak realization.
Pain, fear, injury, fatigue and death are experientially real. A lethal
failure can end the active realization; Valnak restores/reinstantiates
from preserved state. Memory remains.

This is survivable preparation for mistakes that would be permanently
fatal against ELDRIS outside. Veterans learn to function through fear
and trauma rather than becoming magically immune to them.

## Persistent residence

After the First Trial, every participant awakens in a **persistent
personal residence**. The same residence remains theirs across cycles;
return 23 years later and it is still home.

Residences are cheaply customizable with credits: forest/stream,
island/beach, other landscapes, or city-integrated apartments. Basic
residences need not occupy ordinary public geography. The front door is
an addressable transition into public Valnak.

## Node and HUD

Every entrant receives a personal **node**. Early on it remains visibly
present and the entrant deliberately thinks/focuses at it. Familiarity
becomes like learning a new muscle: neurological/energetic entrainment
makes control intuitive; experienced users can let it recede/passive or
call it active.

Current established HUD functions: - Store - Auction House - Map -
Messages

This list is intentionally expandable.

Outside combat the HUD may be fully active. In combat/dungeons it
normally recedes/passivates to avoid distraction, but the participant
can call up a dungeon map.

Node interfaces are advanced spatial displays, not literal Earth
computers. Intent is the primary input.

## Store and Main Shop

Node Store and immersive Main Shop access the same Valnak catalog. The
Main Shop can manifest as a dark/black space with a huge
horizon-to-horizon, floor-to-ceiling wall of rectangular shaping/Binding
possibilities. Search by intent reorganizes the catalog.

A normal first-time combatant has **1,000 credits** and may build a
starter Transduction System, e.g. a 300-credit Fireball plus a
700-credit shield.

## Auction House and Creation economy

Valnak rewards human craftsmanship because it is training/growing
craftspeople, not because Valnak lacks armor.

Normal loop: stock -\> human craft -\> Valnak evaluation -\> credits -\>
recycle object to stock -\> next craftsperson.

Most armor is recycled back to metal stock. Exceptional human artifacts
may be **kept**, enhanced by Valnak, and placed in the Auction House.
Enhancements can include weightlessness, fire resistance,
self-repair/geometry memory, etc. Being selected is a major craft
distinction.

Human artistry matters even though Valnak is vastly capable; capability
is not identical to individual human creativity.

## City instancing / occupancy architecture

Valnak can support billions without crowding one city. A canonical
utopian city topology can be instantiated repeatedly: City A, B, C...
AA, etc. Instances share the same major layout/services but have
different occupants. Valnak caps comfortable occupancy and routes people
dynamically.

A person has a default city instance. Leaving their residence normally
routes their door to a hub in that city. They may select another
destination/city on the Map, subject to capacity.

Groups formed through the node can be routed to a mutually available
city instance/hub. Valnak may instantiate a new city copy when useful.
The reader need only understand that grouping lets people meet and
Valnak prevents overcrowding.

## First-cycle gating

First-cycle participants are cohort-gated for their entire first
seven-season experience. They live/train/group/dungeon with other
first-cycle entrants and cannot be carried by ancient Violet/White
veterans. After the first cycle, later returns open mature Valnak.

## City visual constitution

Valnak city is NOT an Earth-city model. Visual law: **glass + metal +
water + living landscape + open plazas/open space**.

Reference feeling: - advanced transparent/glass architecture - elegant
metal - towers integrated with gardens - large open plazas - abundant
streams, pools, waterfalls, lakes - bridges, terraces, atria -
vegetation integrated at ground and vertical levels - no infrastructure
ugliness as a design driver - architecture and nature do not compete for
land - public space can be extravagant because Valnak solves occupancy
by instantiation - city can be underpopulated relative to physical
capacity - residences largely do not consume public-city acreage unless
specifically city-integrated

Exact city mileage/layout/hub count remains OPEN. Do not reintroduce
conventional zoning/road-grid assumptions unless explicitly chosen
later.
