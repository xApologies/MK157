## Kira

Kira is 22 at opening, seven days before the predictable Valnak opening.
She was born roughly one month after the previous closing. Society and
Kira believe she is Afflicted; genuine Afflicted children usually die
around 11--17, so she believes reaching Valnak is her only chance to
live.

Phenotype: white/fair skin, healthy white hair, deep steel-gray eyes.
Unlike true Afflicted pathology, she has no brittle hair, progressive
hardening, Genesis calcification-like buildup, or degeneration.

Hidden author truth: Kira is **not Afflicted**. Let `P = Princess`;
`P_Black = Kira`. She is the unique/Prime Princess of Black at author
level. Nobody in-world knows this, including Elara. Kira is genuinely
human, not a cosmic being pretending to be one.

Father: unknown dungeon delver from a brief relationship with Kira's
mother. Mother: Village Hub apothecary and friend of the local
armorsmith's wife. Around age eight, a localized infectious outbreak
killed her mother. The smithing family took Kira in through a mix of
compassion and useful work.

Smith household: master armorsmith father with Crown contract; mother
handles business/household; older son in 30s; younger son around Kira's
age; daughter \~17, sister-like to Kira and involved in
logistics/deliveries. They pay Kira fairly and house/feed her, but she
remains socially constrained.

Kira is taboo more than universally abused. Adults often avoid/ignore
her, cross streets, stop talking, or make protective gestures.
Children/peers can be cruel, including taunting and occasional beatings.
The smithing family and a coffee-shop barista are among her few normal
relationships. She plays **raeon**, owns a tiny Genesis Card collection
with two Fireball Genesis Cards, and wants a third.

### Mail armorsmith

At \~8 she begins basic repetitive mail labor: wire -\> straighten/pull
-\> mandrel -\> coil -\> remove -\> clip rings -\> repeat. Over fourteen
years she advances through closures, weaving, repairs, full garments,
fit and articulation. At 22 she is effectively the best mail armorsmith
in the Village Hub region: fast turnaround, uniform rings, precise
weave, seamless repairs, excellent fit, low waste, extreme dexterity.
She loves mail as a puzzle of links forming one protective surface.

She lacks a Valnak Binding, so she cannot perform full Transductive
blacksmithing. A true master blacksmith may never touch a tool:
alchemically enhanced stock is conducted through
Binding/shaping/Transduction into armor or weapons. Craft ladder is
roughly apprentice -\> journeyman -\> blacksmith -\> expert -\> master,
with increasing replacement of manual tools by direct matter conduction.

Kira is physically strong from smithy labor: ingots, stock, armor,
tools, deliveries; dexterous from mail. Her Valnak plan is: survive/get
healed, choose Creation, become a true Transductive blacksmith.

## Starscape and Black

Use **starscape** for the local realized cosmological domain. Other
starscapes may exist but do not interact materially in this novel; each
has its own Valnak.

Author singularity centers: Red=1/7, Orange=2/7, Yellow=3/7, Green=4/7,
Blue=5/7, Violet=6/7. These superpose as White to realize a starscape.
`neon` is the realized position/state within a basin, not the basin
identity itself.

Kira's starscape has an unusually displaced/advanced Yellow-neon
configuration, perturbing the White superposition and contributing to
Genesis instability: neon disequilibrium -\> Genesis-field instability
-\> recursive Genesis collapse -\> ELDRIS. Civilization does not know
this root cause.

Black is not an eighth color or rank. Authorially it is
generative/upstream of the six basin singularities. Kira is its living
conscious realization.

Valnak exists beyond the ordinary physical/energy boundary of the
starscape---the author intuition is "the other side of the
table/tree/existence." The Planck scale is a fictional boundary
metaphor, not a claim about real physics.
