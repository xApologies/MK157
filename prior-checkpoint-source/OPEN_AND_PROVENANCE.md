## Key accepted design logic

-   Valnak trains civilization through feedback: civilization acts -\>
    provenance -\> Valnak learns -\> Paths update -\> humans train -\>
    humans return stronger.
-   Paths heavily weight demonstrated aptitude and civilizational need;
    free will is preserved at acceptance.
-   First-cycle gating prevents power-leveling by ancient veterans.
-   Combat Trial rank is useful social credential for dungeon grouping.
-   Human craft objects are mostly recycled; exceptional works can be
    Valnak-enhanced and auctioned.
-   Nobility's wealth produces better Valnak preparation, stronger
    Transduction, longer lives, and a distinct temporal/political class.
-   Kira's first Yellow is remarkable because the other Yellow arrived
    with elite lifelong preparation and equipment while Kira arrived
    with nothing.
-   Kira's armor is the integrated battle system through which her
    broader Black/Genesis access will progressively express.
