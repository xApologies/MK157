# MK157 Live Model --- Systems, Trials, Paths, World, Story State

## Transduction core

Inherited simplified MK-147 engine: **Binding -\> PSSP projection -\>
basin access/energy -\> energized field topology -\> concept -\>
conscious collapse -\> shaping.**

Binding and active shaping are distinct. PSSP is the projected/manifold
scaffold. Concept is a major controllable variable; better concept
improves realization, efficiency, stability/control and energy cost.

There is NO mana. Fatigue is physiological/endurance-like loss of
sustainable Genesis-field/basin access. The basin still exists; the
person becomes unable to maintain access. Rest restores it. Training
improves endurance/efficiency.

## Six Transductive Domains

1.  Persistent Topology / Stabilization / Decoherence.
2.  Dimensional Geometry.
3.  Genesis Field Topology.
4.  Genesis Expression: Genesis energy and ordinary matter/energy
    expression; earth/air/fire/water prime-element concepts,
    periodic-table interactions, beams, Fireball, plasma/lightning
    channels.
5.  Resonance / Persistence.
6.  Chirality / Structural Admissibility.

## Five classes

-   augmenter
-   helkir
-   summoner
-   maege
-   architect

Classes are specializations across the six domains, not one-to-one basin
labels. Architects directly organize Genesis-field topology and create
**velis**, localized temporarily persistent Genesis-field topologies.
Helkir focus on biological stabilization/coherence. Augmenters center on
resonance/persistence and bodily enhancement. Maegi center on Genesis
Expression. Summoner mechanics remain open enough not to force MK-147
Kira's old implementation into MK157.

## Creation

Creation is separate from combat class identity though it uses
Transduction. Main families: - Craft (blacksmithing, armorsmithing,
etc.) - ru'ne work / inscription/artificing (final profession noun
open) - Alchemy

`ru’ne` are structural marks/language of the Genesis Field; sigils are
formalized operational ru'ne structures. Ru'ne can participate in
fields, matter growth, spatial locking, armor properties, etc. Alchemy
is material science. `vaen` are naturally occurring unusual fantasy
resources/materials (Voidbloom Lotus, Deadwood branch, etc.) used
especially in Alchemy/material work. MK157 does NOT use vaen as the
personal Binding source; Valnak handles Bindings.

Master blacksmithing is Transductive matter conduction. A master can
take alchemically enhanced stock and shape/heat/form it through Bindings
and shapings without conventional tools. Mail is a notable manually
viable domain, explaining Kira's pre-Valnak mastery.

## Credits and Paths

First-time participants receive **1,000 credits** for courage/commitment
to participation.

Credits buy Bindings/shapings/equipment/services/items.
Prestige/recognition can be distinct from spendable credits.

A **Path** is a Valnak-curated specialization sequence with discounted
shapings and checkpoints. Path = economic advantage + reduced build
freedom.

A participant may reject a Path and buy freely at normal prices. Once a
Path is accepted, the cultural/practical advice is to finish its
checkpoints before diverging. After completion, a person can take
another Path. Paths can compose: Fire + Water may later support Steam,
etc.

Two dominant weights on Path availability: 1. **individual demonstrated
aptitude** 2. **civilizational need** Personal desire matters, but
acceptance is where free will governs. Valnak incentivizes rather than
drafts.

Valnak continuously updates Paths using human provenance, new concepts,
ELDRIS behavior, successes/failures, and the state of civilization.
Humanity teaches Valnak while Valnak trains humanity.

Illustrative Fire Path: independent Fireball + Fire Shield + Flamewake
might total 2,300 credits, while a strong Path offer could package them
for 1,000. Exact prices/discount percentages are provisional unless
story-locking requires them.

Path colors Red--White indicate quality/strength/value of the offer and
specialization architecture. Exact universal discount table remains
OPEN.

## First Trial architecture

Everyone undergoes a First Trial to establish demonstrated competence.

Top level: - Creation - Combat

Combat functions: - Combatant: maege, summoner, augmenter - Support:
helkir, architect

Combatants defeat escalating ELDRIS waves. Helkir support trial: sustain
three simulated combatants through escalating battlefield damage.
Architect support trial: keep the battlefield/team survivable through
velis, barriers, field topology/control. Creation trials evaluate craft
progression rather than kills; author pattern can be reproduce -\>
repair -\> construct -\> adapt -\> solve -\> create. Exact non-Kira
trial details need not be exhaustive.

The solo Combat Trial is available throughout Valnak, not just first
entry. A participant can retest as their Transduction System develops.

Wave counts use prime escalation, with Wave 1 = 1, then consecutive
primes: W1 1 W2 3 W3 5 W4 7 W5 11 W6 13 W7 17 W8 19 W9 23 W10 29 W11 31
W12 37 W13 41 W14 43 W15 47 W16 53 W17 59 W18 61 W19 67 W20 71 W21 73

Cumulative through W8 = 76. W9 adds 23. Cumulative through W23 = 873.

Working Combat Rank thresholds: - Red: waves 1--3 - Orange: waves 4--7 -
Yellow: waves 8--12 - Green: waves 13--18 - Blue: waves 19--25 - Violet:
waves 26--34 - White: wave 35+

White is extraordinarily rare; only a few historical combatants have
reached it. White may itself support recursive/internal color prestige
later; details not needed now.

Rank is the highest solo wave successfully cleared. It is a standardized
demonstrated-combat credential used for group formation. Difficult
dungeons may recruit Green+ etc. Rank answers "how capable?" while
class/system/experience answer "capable of what?"

Valnak permits entrants to bring owned armor, weapons, ru'ne equipment,
Alchemical preparations, constructs, etc. The trial measures actual
demonstrated capability, not naked innate talent.

Most first-time entrants rank Red or Orange. First-trial Yellow is rare,
perhaps one or two people in an ordinary cycle, and usually involves
years of preparation plus expensive equipment.

Combat staging because her entry makes Black possibilities accessible.

Instead of the normal immense catalog, Elara presents Kira with **six**
unprecedented options, each costing her full 1,000 credits and each

1.  **Armor of the Abyss** --- Kira-specific integrated liquid-Black
    armor/battle-system topology.
2.  **Blackstar** --- maege analogue; condensed/projected Black
    Genesis-energy orb/offensive expression.
3.  **Nightwyrm** --- summoner analogue; Black draconic combat
    manifestation.
4.  **Overstate** --- augmenter analogue; resolves body into an
    otherwise inadmissible enhanced resonance state.

6.  **Blackweave** --- architect analogue; direct localized Black

These are NOT "void magic." Black permits neon/basin configurations
ordinarily inadmissible. Valnak has never had access to these

Kira chooses **Armor of the Abyss** because she is an armorsmith.

## Armor of the Abyss --- initial state

Armor is Kira's integrated battle system, not merely protective
clothing.

Visual: - seamless/body-conformal liquid Black energy - Black has depth
like looking into deep dark water/abyss - subtle
Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet neon geometry/color occasionally
shimmers far beneath the Black surface - not painted rainbow; colors
appear buried/latent - active topology rather than conventional metal -
no straps/buckles required

Initial capability: - armor flows over/encompasses Kira - dynamic
self-restoration/re-resolution after disruption - protects against
attacks that would normally maim - three claws per hand: - claw 1
aligned with thumb - claw 2 formed over/through index + middle - claw 3
formed over/through ring + pinky - her biological fingers remain intact;
the projected armor geometry forms the three larger claws

Kira's root author capability is full/unprecedented domain over
Genesis-field manipulation, but NOT unlimited power. Best phrasing:
**unrestricted access/manipulation, finite expression**.
Authority/access is enormous; capacity, fatigue, concept, skill,
control, and experience remain bounded.

At first her realized vocabulary is tiny: Armor + three-claw
configuration. She is not immediately omnipotent.

Armor repair is not biological healing. Damage disrupts the topology;
the intended armor state re-resolves. Repeated restoration imposes
fatigue/throughput burden. Momentum can still throw/disorient her.

## Kira First Combat Trial

Kira has: - no combat career - no prior Binding - no dungeon
experience - no combat equipment brought in - no consumables - no
meaningful combat training - Armor of the Abyss acquired minutes earlier

She fights as an inelegant brawler/juggernaut: close distance,
hack/slash, take hits, keep moving. Her smithy strength/dexterity help,
but she is NOT a combat genius.

She clears **Wave 8**, defeating 76 cumulative ELDRIS, and therefore
earns the lowest Yellow rank: **Kira --- Yellow, Wave 8**.

She fails Wave 9 (23 ELDRIS) by saturation. The mass buries her; the
armor is attacked faster than it can stabilize/re-resolve; breaches
allow claws/teeth through; she suffers severe trauma and dies. She later
wakes restored in her residence.

Her reaction is unusual: she understands the horror/pain yet wants to
improve/return. She enjoys the fight/problem, not the pain. This marks
her as psychologically unusual to veterans and Elara.

Armor of the Abyss is essentially Kira's first/entry shaping; the
alarming implication is what it may become as it develops.

## Shaping rank and longevity

Individual shapings develop Red -\> Orange -\> Yellow -\> Green -\> Blue
-\> Violet -\> White.

A person's broad/general shaping-development level reflects the
quality/depth of their attachment to the Genesis Field and corresponding
biological restructuring. Higher development tends to prolong life.

Working average life expectancy heuristic: - Red \~100 years - Orange
\~130 - Yellow \~170 - Green \~210 - Blue \~280 - Violet \~330 - White
\~400

These are averages, not expiration dates. Reader-facing explanation can
remain simple: stronger Genesis-field attachment / higher general
shaping development -\> longer life.

This creates powerful incentives. Some people endure Valnak combat
primarily to extend lifespan. Multi-cycle veterans can be 100--300+
years old; extreme White-scale figures can approach \~400.

Nobility has a structural advantage because wealthy houses train youth
for Valnak from childhood, supply equipment/instruction, gain power and
longevity, and can therefore operate on a different temporal/political
scale.
